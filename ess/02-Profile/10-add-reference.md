# Add a reference

Reference records are submitted through ESS and go through the approval workflow before being confirmed on your profile.

1. Open the References tab

   From your **Full Profile**, click the **References** tab.

2. Add a new reference

   Click **Add new reference**.

3. Enter the referee's details

   Fill in the required fields: referee name, organisation, and your relationship to them.

   ![The Add New Reference form showing the referee name, organisation, and relationship fields](./images/add-reference-1.png)

4. Submit for approval

   Click **Submit** to send the reference for review.
