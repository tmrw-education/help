# Add professional experience

Previous experience records are submitted through ESS and go through the approval workflow before being confirmed on your profile.

1. Open the Professional experience tab

   From your **Full Profile**, click the **Professional experience** tab.

2. Add a new experience

   Click **Add new experience +**.

3. Enter the Employer's details

   Fill in the required fields: Employer's name, your position, and the company's contact details.

   ![The Add New experience form showing the Employer's name, position, and start/end date fields](./images/add-professiona-experience-1.png)

4. Submit for approval

   Click **Submit** to send the reference for review.
