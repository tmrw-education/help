# Complete the offboarding survey

The Offboarding Survey is assigned to you as part of the offboarding process when you are preparing to leave the organisation. It captures your feedback and experience, and is included in your offboarding checklist.

1. Open the Questionnaires tab

   From the ESS portal, go to **Employment ▸ Questionnaires** or click the **Questionnaires** tab.

2. Find the Offboarding Survey

   The survey appears in your questionnaire list with an **Assigned** status.

3. Start the survey

   Click on the Offboarding Survey and click **Start**.

4. Answer the questions

   Work through the exit survey questions, providing honest feedback about your experience.

   ![The Offboarding Survey open in ESS showing exit feedback questions with rating and free-text fields](./images/complete-offboarding-survey-1.png)

5. Submit

   Click **Submit** (or **Done**) to finalise your response.

6. Mark your checklist task complete

   After submitting the survey, return to your onboarding/offboarding task list and mark the associated task as **complete** to update the status for HR.
