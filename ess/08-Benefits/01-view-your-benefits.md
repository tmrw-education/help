# View your benefits

Your benefits statement is visible directly in ESS. This is a read-only view — your entitlements are set up and maintained by HR and the Finance team.

1. Open the Benefits section

   From the ESS portal, navigate to the **Benefits** section.

2. Review your benefits

   Your benefits statement displays the entitlements associated with your employment. Review the values shown.

   ![The Benefits section in ESS showing the employee's entitlements in a read-only view](./images/view-your-benefits-1.png)

   If you have questions about your benefits or believe something is incorrect, contact your HR team directly.
