# View Eldest Child in Family

The Eldest Child in Family report identifies the eldest sibling in each enrolled family. Use this to confirm sibling order before applying family-based fee discounts.

1. Open the report

   From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules ▸ Academic Management**, expand **Inquiries and reports**, expand **Students and families**, and click **Eldest child in family**.

2. Review the data

   The report lists the eldest sibling per family (④). Use this to confirm sibling order before running fee batches or applying family discounts.
