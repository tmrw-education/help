# Configure an Advance Payment Discount — Date Based

In this setup, the discount is calculated based on the payment date only. If payment is received on or before the eligible date, the system applies the predefined discount regardless of the payment method.

1. Open Cash discounts and create a new entry

   From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules ▸ Accounts receivable**, expand **Payment setup**, and click **Cash discounts**. Click **New** and complete the following:

   - **Cash Discount** code — enter a unique code.
   - **Description** — enter a description.
   - **Discount Percentage** or **Fixed Discount Amount** — specify one or the other.
   - **Main Account** — assign the account where the discount will be posted.

2. Save

   Click **Save**.
