# Process a Payment with an Early Payment Discount

The system automatically applies the discount when the payment date is before the discount due date.

1. Create the payment journal

   From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules ▸ Accounts receivable**, expand **Payments**, and click **Customer payment journal**. Click **New**, select the **Name** (④), then click **Lines** in the Action Pane (⑤).

   ![Process a Payment with an Early Payment Discount — the payment journal with Lines highlighted in the Action Pane](./images/process-payment-with-discount-1.png)

2. Settle the invoice with the discount

   Select the fee payer (⑥) and click **Settle transactions** (⑦). Set the payment **Due date** to a date before the discount due date (⑧). Settle the invoice(s) by clicking **OK** (⑨) — the system automatically applies the discount, reducing the payable amount. Enter the **Payment reference** (⑩), then click **Post** (⑪).

   ![Process a Payment with an Early Payment Discount — the Settle transactions dialog](./images/process-payment-with-discount-2.png)

   ![Process a Payment with an Early Payment Discount —  The payment due date set before the discount due date](./images/process-payment-with-discount-3.png)

   ![Process a Payment with an Early Payment Discount — the journal line after settling showing the reduced payable amount](./images/process-payment-with-discount-4.png)

3. Verify the discount

   Check the voucher to confirm the discount was posted. Click the three dots in the toolbar and choose **All related vouchers** to see the discount amount.
