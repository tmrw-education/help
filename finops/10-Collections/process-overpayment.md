# Process an Overpayment

When a fee payer pays more than the invoice total at the counter, the system captures the excess in the **Overpayment** field. The cashier can review and correct the amount before posting.

1. Create the cashier receipt

   From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules ▸ Accounts receivable**, expand **Payments**, and click **Cashier receipt**. Click **+ Cashier receipt** and complete the following:

   - **Customer** — enter the student account.
   - **Invoice** — tick the invoice to be paid in the panel on the right.
   - **Method of payment** — select the payment method.
   - **Amount** — enter an amount greater than the invoice total. After saving, the excess amount appears in the **Overpayment** field, allowing the cashier to review and correct before posting.
   - **Payment reference** — enter a reference if required.

2. Post and deliver the receipt

   Click **Post**. Enable the **Print receipt** toggle if a copy is required. Click **OK**.
