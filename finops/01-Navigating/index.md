# Navigating F&O

Finance and Operations (F&O) is the financial backbone of the tmrw platform, delivered through Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations. Because F&O sits on the same unified data layer as Student Management and HR, financial transactions are connected directly to student and family records — fee generation, payment processing, scholarship approval, and statement production flow through one source of truth, with pro-rata adjustments, sibling discounts, split billing, and audit trails handled automatically by the platform.
