# View All Students' Visa Types

The **Students with visa** report gives a full list of students with active visa records across the school. Use it to review international student cohort data and filter by visa status.

1. Open the report

   From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules ▸ Academic Management**, expand **Inquiries and reports**, expand **Visa Management**, and click **Students with visa**.

2. Filter and review

   Use the checkboxes (④) to filter results by visa status. The report lists all students with active visa records and their visa type.
