## What you’ll do
Set up the sibling order in Academic Management, including defining the number of children per family and assigning sibling order numbers. These values identify each student’s position within their family for discount purposes.

---

## Sibling order setup

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup** and click **Sibling order**.
3. Click **New** to create sibling order entries.
4. In the first column, enter the **Sibling order** number.
5. In the second column, enter the **Description**.  
   - **Note:** For each possible child in a family, assign a sibling order number (e.g., `1` for first child, `2` for second, up to your maximum, such as `9`). Use `0` for students who are not yet current (e.g., future students or those transitioning between programs).
6. Click **Save**.
7. Go back to **Modules** from the sidebar.
8. Select **Sales and marketing**.
9. Expand **Prices and discounts** and click **Customer price/discount groups**.
10. In the **Show** field, change the value to **Line discount group**.
11. Click **New** to assign the correct sibling order.
12. Enter the sibling order number in the **Price groups** column (e.g., `1`, `2`, `3`).
13. Name the entry in the next column (e.g., *First Child*, *Second Child*).
14. In the **Sibling** column, use the dropdown and match this with the sibling order.
15. Repeat as many times as required.
16. Click **Save**.

## Trade agreement

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Sales and marketing**.
2. Expand **Prices and discounts** and click **Trade agreement journals**.
3. Click **New**.
4. In the **Name** column, select **Sibling discount**.
5. Change view to **Lines**.
6. Change **Party code type** to **Group**.
7. Set **Account selection** to the number of siblings (e.g., *First child*).
8. Change **Product code type** to **Group**.
9. Set **Item relation** to **Sibling discount**.
10. In **Details**, set the policy **Start date** and **End date**.  
    - **Note:** Leave the end date blank to run the discount indefinitely.
11. Enter the discount amount in **Discount percentage 1** (e.g., `10.00` for `10%`).
12. Click **New** to add the second child discount; repeat these steps as needed.
13. Click **Save**.
14. Click **Post** to activate the trade agreement policy.
15. Go back to **Modules** from the sidebar.
16. Select **Product information management**.
17. Expand **Products** and click **Released products**.
18. Open each tuition fee item.
19. Expand the **Sell** section.
20. Assign the **Line discount group** by editing the field and choosing the **Sibling discount** trade agreement.
21. Click **Save**.
22. Repeat for other tuition fee items.

## Sibling order calculation

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Fee schedules** and click **All fee schedules**.
3. Open the relevant template(s) and ensure all tuition fee items are correctly listed.
4. Go back to **Modules** from the sidebar.
5. Select **Academic Management**.
6. Expand **Periodic tasks** and click **Generate sales order batch processing**.
7. In the dialog, enter a **Batch description** (e.g., `School Fee Invoice Term 1`).
8. Expand **Records to Include (Customers)** and select the customer group (e.g., all students in **Academic Year 1**).
9. Expand **Records to Include (Fee schedule templates)** and choose the correct fee schedule template.
10. Click **OK** to run the task in the background and generate sales orders for all selected students.
11. When the task completes, go back to **Modules** from the sidebar.
12. Select **Academic Management**.
13. Expand **Fee schedule batches** and click **All fee schedule batches**.
14. Open the latest **Batch** and review the **Sales orders**.
