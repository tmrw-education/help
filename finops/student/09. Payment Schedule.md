# Payment Schedule Setup

## What you’ll do
Set up and manage payment schedules to divide invoices into multiple installments. This includes creating and configuring installment plans and applying these schedules to payment options for streamlined installment-based payments.

---

## Create a new payment schedule

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Accounts receivable**.
2. Expand **Payments setup** and click **Payment schedules**.
3. Click **New** to create a new payment schedule entry.
4. Enter a unique **Code** and a **Description** for the payment schedule.
5. Assign the payment **Allocation**.
6. Assign the timeline in the **Payments per** field.
7. Enter the frequency in the **Change** field.
8. Enter the **Number of payments**.
9. Click **Save**.

---

## Apply the payment schedule to payment plans

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup**, then expand **Payment option setup**.
3. Click **New** to create a new payment option.
4. Select the required **Payment plan**.
5. Enter an **Option name** and a **Description**.
6. Set the **Start** and **End dates** for the payment plan.
7. Click **Save**.
