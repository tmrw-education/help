# In-Person Customer Payments (Over the Counter)

## What you’ll do
Process and post customer payments made directly at the school’s counter. This includes selecting the fee payer, choosing the payment method, allocating payments to invoices, handling advance payments, posting the payment journal, and managing receipts.

---

## Steps

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Accounts receivable**.
2. Expand **Payments** and click **Over the counter payment**.
3. Click **Create New Customer Payment**.
4. A new form appears for entering payment details. Choose the **Fee payer** from the list.  
   - The system displays the fee payer’s name and any open invoices.
5. Select the payment method (e.g., credit card or cash) from the **Method of payment** column.
6. Enter the **Amount** being paid and select the **Payment account** where the funds will be deposited.
7. Mark the invoices the payment should be applied to.  
   - **Note:** If the payment exceeds the invoice total, the extra amount is recorded as an advance.
8. The system automatically checks the **Pay in Advance** box if there is an unallocated amount.
9. Click **Post** to finalize the entry.  
   - The system generates a unique **Journal number** for reference.
10. A dialog box appears asking if you want to print and/or email the receipt. Select your options and click **OK**.
11. The system prints the receipt and/or sends it to the fee payer’s email as specified.
12. Click **Back** to return to the payment screen.
13. To review the posted entry, copy the **Journal number** in the top left of the window.
14. Click **Back** to return to **Over the counter payments**.
15. Change the **Show** dropdown to **Posted**.
16. Search for the journal number by entering it in the **Journal batch number** column filter.
17. Open the journal to view details or the voucher for accounting entries.
18. Confirm that:
    - The total payment is deposited in the selected bank account.
    - Invoices are settled.
    - Any advance is recorded.

# Scheduled Credit Card

## What you’ll do
Handle customer fee payments via credit card within the academic management system. This includes generating payment proposals, selecting and authorizing invoices for payment, and posting completed transactions.

---

## Steps

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Accounts receivable**.
2. Expand **Payments** and click **Customer payment journal**.
3. Click **New** to create a new journal and select the appropriate **Journal name**.
4. Click **Lines** in the toolbar.
5. Click the **Payment Proposal** button, then select **Create Payment Proposal**.
6. In the dialog box, enter the **Date range** for due invoices (e.g., `January 1 to January 31`).
7. Set the **Method of payment** to `Credit card (CC)`.
8. Set the **Summarised payment date**.
9. Click **OK** to run the proposal.
10. The system displays all invoices due within the selected period. Select the invoices to be paid, or leave all unmarked to select all.
11. Click **Create Payments** to transfer selected invoices to the payment journal.
12. Delete any lines with zero or debit amounts, as only payable invoices should remain.
13. Enter the **Payment reference** as required.
14. Click the **Function** button in the toolbar, then select **Generate Credit Card Payments**.  
    - **Note:** The system requests authorization from the service provider. If successful, the status changes to **Approved**; if not, a failure message appears and unauthorized lines are deleted.
15. Once authorization is received, click **Post**.  
    - **Note:** The system confirms posting, and the **Post** button becomes inactive. All transactions are now posted and complete.
