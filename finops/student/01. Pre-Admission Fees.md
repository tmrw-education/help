# Pre-admission Fees Setup

## What you’ll do

Set up pre‑admission fee transaction types and posting logic, then update fee schedule parameters to enable pre‑admission processing.

---

## Pre-admission type creation

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.
3. Click **Pre-admission types**.
![](https://github.com/Tmrw-Education/finops/blob/423977539a2fdbaf3cd1dd5d996dd1a5d5dd378f/FNO%20UG%20Preadmission%201.png)
4. Click **New** in the toolbar.
5. In **Transaction type**, enter `Application fee`.
6. In **Description**, enter `Application fee`.
7. Repeat to create `Enrolment fee` and `Enrolment deposit`, entering these in both **Transaction type** and **Description**.
8. Click **Save**.
![](https://github.com/Tmrw-Education/finops/blob/main/FNO%20UG%20Preadmission%202.png)

---

## Set up posting logic for each transaction type

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.
3. Click **Pre-admission posting**.
4. Click **New** in the toolbar.
5. Set **Transaction type** to `Application fee`.
6. Set **Account entry** to `General journal`.  
   - **Note:** **Account entry** determines which fields must be completed next.
7. Complete the entry by selecting values for **Journal name**, **Main account**, **Posting profile**, and **Receipt journal name**.
8. For `Application fee`, set **Journal name** to `APF`.
9. Set **Main account** to `Other Income`.
10. Click **New** to add another entry.
11. Set **Transaction type** to `Enrolment fee`.
12. Set **Account entry** to `Free text invoice`.
13. Set **Main account** to `Other Income`.
14. Set **Posting profile** to `General posting`.
15. Set **Receipt journal name** to `Enrolment fee payment`.
16. Click **New** to add another entry.
17. Set **Transaction type** to `Enrolment deposit`.
18. Set **Account entry** to `Sales order`.
19. Set **Posting profile** to `General posting`.
20. Set **Receipt journal name** to `Enrolment deposit payment`.
21. Click **Save**.

---

## Update fee schedule parameters

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup** and click **Fee schedule parameters**.
3. In the left pane, open **General**.
4. Under **Enrolment deposit**, set **Enrolment deposit sales category** to `Enrolment deposit`.
5. Click **Save**.

# Pre-admission Fees Enrolment Transactions

## What you’ll do

Manually add and post application fees, enrolment fees, and enrolment deposits in the F&O system when integration with the CE system is not yet available.

---

## Manually adding Application Fee records

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.
3. Click **All pre-admission fees**.
4. Click **New** in the toolbar.
5. Enter the **Date and time** in the **Integration date and time** field.
6. Choose **Application fee** from the **Transaction type** dropdown.
7. Enter the **Transaction number** if one has not been generated by CE.
8. Enter the **Student name**.  
   - **Note:** Student account is not required because the student is not yet in the system.
9. Enter the **Amount**.
10. Click **Post** in the toolbar.  
    - The status updates from **Created** to **Paid** once the payment is successful.
11. Click **Save**.

---

## Manually adding Enrolment Fee records

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.
3. Click **All pre-admission fees**.
4. Click **New** in the toolbar.
5. Enter the **Date and time** in the **Integration date and time** field.
6. Choose **Enrolment fee** from the **Transaction type** dropdown.
7. Enter the **Transaction number** if one has not been generated by CE.
8. Search for the student in the **Student account** dropdown.  
   - This automatically populates the **Student name** field.
9. Enter the **Amount**.
10. Click **Post** in the toolbar.  
    - The status updates from **Created** to **Posted**.
11. Click **Save**.

---

## Manually adding Enrolment Deposit records

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.
3. Click **All pre-admission fees**.
4. Click **New** in the toolbar.
5. Enter the **Date and time** in the **Integration date and time** field.
6. Choose **Enrolment deposit** from the **Transaction type** dropdown.
7. Enter the **Transaction number** if one has not been generated by CE.
8. Search for the student in the **Student account** dropdown.  
   - This automatically populates the **Student name** field.
9. Choose the **Academic year** for the student.
10. Enter the **Amount**.
11. Click **Post** in the toolbar.  
    - The status updates from **Created** to **Posted**.
12. Click **Save**.

# Pre-admission Fee Payments

**What you’ll do**  
Process enrolment fee and deposit payments directly from the Pre-admission Fees table in the Academic Management system. This is used when payments are made over the counter, rather than through the Parent Portal (PXP).

---

## Pre-admission enrolment fee payment process

1. From the FNO dashboard, open **Modules** from the sidebar.  
2. Select **Academic Management**.  
3. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.  
4. Click **All pre-admission fees**.  
5. Identify the student by clicking the **Student name** column and using the filter to search (e.g., Stuart Little).  
6. Check either the **Enrolment fee** and/or **Enrolment deposit** from the far-left column.  
7. Click **Post receipt** in the toolbar.  
8. Choose the appropriate **Bank account** from the dropdown.  
9. Decide whether to enable the review option:  
   a. If **Enabled**, review payment channels manually before processing.  
   b. If **Disabled**, the system posts the payment automatically.  
10. Click **OK** to finalise the payment.  
11. Click **Save** in the toolbar.

# Pre-admission Fees Refundable Deposits

**What you’ll do**  
Process enrolment deposit refunds and forfeits within the Academic Management system. Only deposits with a status of **Received** are eligible for refund or forfeit.

---

## Forfeiting a deposit

1. From the FNO dashboard, open **Modules** from the sidebar.  
2. Select **Academic Management**.  
3. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.  
4. Click **Pre-admission deposits**.  
5. When the deposit criteria window opens, click **OK** to view all deposits.  
6. Check the deposit to be forfeited from the far-left column.  
7. Click **Forfeit** in the toolbar.  
8. In the dialog box on the right, choose whether to **Preview** or disable the toggle to post automatically.  
9. To preview, click the **Forfeit journal number**.  
10. Open the journal by clicking **Lines** in the toolbar.  
11. Review the transaction, select the credit line using the checkbox, and click **Post**.  
12. Click **Save** in the toolbar.

---

## Refunding a deposit

1. From the FNO dashboard, open **Modules** from the sidebar.  
2. Select **Academic Management**.  
3. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.  
4. Click **Pre-admission deposits**.  
5. When the deposit criteria window opens, click **OK** to view all deposits.  
6. Check the deposit to be refunded from the far-left column.  
7. Click **Refund** in the toolbar.  
8. Choose the **Bank account** for the refund.  
9. In the dialog box on the right, choose whether to **Preview** or disable the toggle to post automatically.  
10. To preview, click the **Refund journal number**.  
11. Open the journal by clicking **Lines** in the toolbar.  
12. Review the transaction, select the credit line using the checkbox, and click **Post**.  
13. Click **Save** in the toolbar.

# Pre-admission Fees Reversing Unpaid Fees

**What you’ll do**  
Reverse or cancel posted enrolment fees and deposits in the Academic Management system.

---

## Reverse enrolment deposit or fee

1. From the FNO dashboard, open **Modules** from the sidebar.  
2. Select **Academic Management**.  
3. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Pre-admission fees**.  
4. Click **All pre-admission fees**.  
5. Filter the view by **Posted** entries by clicking the **Status** column and selecting **Posted**.  
6. Select the enrolment deposit or fee using the checkbox on the far-left column, then click **Cancel** in the toolbar.  
7. In the dialog box on the right, choose the **Reverse posting date**, then click **OK**.  
8. Click **Save** in the toolbar.
