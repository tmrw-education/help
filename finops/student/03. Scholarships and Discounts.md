# Manage Scholarships and Discounts

## What you’ll do
Set up and manage scholarships and discounts, link them to fee items and students, handle approval workflows, and view applied discounts.

---

## Scholarships and Discounts navigation

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup** and click **Scholarships and discounts**.

---

## Create a new Scholarship or Discount

1. Click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Enter the **Discount Code**.
3. Enter the **Discount Name**.
4. If the scholarship is active, select the checkbox. Leave blank if inactive.
5. If the scholarship requires approval, select the checkbox and choose a **User Group** of approvers.
6. If approval is not required, leave the checkbox and **User Group** blank.
7. Click **Save**.

---

## Linking scholarships and discounts to fee items

1. Select a scholarship or discount by checking the circle to the left of the item.
2. Click **General** in the toolbar.
3. Choose **Items** under the **Fees** tab.
4. Click **New** to create a new item.
5. Enter the required details.
6. Click **Save**.

---

## Linking scholarships and discounts to students

1. Select a scholarship or discount by checking the circle to the left of the item.
2. Click **General** in the toolbar.
3. Choose **Students** under the **Students** tab.
4. Click **New** to create a new record.
5. Choose a student from the **Student Account** dropdown.
6. The **Student Name**, **Code**, and **Scholarship/Discount Name** will prepopulate.
7. Choose whether to apply a **Percentage** or **Total discount amount**.
8. Set the **Start** and **Expiration date**.
9. Click **Save**.

---

## Scholarship and discount approval process

1. Select the student record in **Draft** status that requires approval.
2. Click the **Approval** dropdown in the toolbar and choose **Report as ready**.
3. The status changes to **Under Review**.
4. Click **Save**.
5. This sends a notification to the approver.

---

## Approving or rejecting scholarships and discounts

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup** and click **Scholarships and discounts**.
3. Select a scholarship or discount by checking the circle to the left of the item.
4. Click **General** in the toolbar.
5. Choose **Students** under the **Students** tab.
6. Select the student whose scholarship/discount requires approval.
7. Click the **Approval** dropdown and choose **Approve** or **Reject**.
8. Click **Save**.

---

## View scholarships and discounts for a student

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Students** and click **All Students**.
3. Search for and select the student by checking the circle to the left of the student ID.
4. Click the **Academic** tab in the toolbar.
5. Under **Related information**, click **Scholarship and discounts**.
6. Use the checkbox to filter by activity.

---

## View approved scholarship and discount fees

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Fee schedule batches** and click **All fee schedule batches**.
3. Search for and open the fee record you want to view.
4. Under **Sales order lines**, scroll to the right to view the discount amount applied.
