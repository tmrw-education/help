# Check Student Master Data

## What you’ll do
Ensure student records are accurate and up to date, including academic year, enrolment dates, sibling order (for family fees), and financial responsibility. These details are required for correct fee calculation and invoice generation.

---

## Check student details

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** from the sidebar.
2. Select **Academic Management**.
3. Expand **Students** and click **All students**.
4. Select a student.
5. Confirm the **Current academic year** under **Enrolment details** and update if needed.
6. From the **Academic** tab in the top toolbar, open **Academic enrolments** under **Related information**.
7. Confirm the **Effective date** and **Expiration date** and update if needed.
8. Click **Back** in the top toolbar.
9. If the student has siblings, confirm the **Sibling** field under **Enrolment details** has a number (e.g., eldest sibling = `1`).
10. In the **Relationships** section, ensure the total **Paid percentage** equals `100.00`.
11. Click **Save** in the top toolbar.

---

## Scholarships and discounts setup

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** from the sidebar.
2. Select **Academic Management**.
3. Expand **Setup** and click **Scholarships and discounts**.
4. Click **New** in the top toolbar.
5. Enter or create a code in the **Code** column.
6. Enter the scholarship or discount name in the **Name** column.
7. Check the **Active** and **Approval** boxes.
8. Assign or create the approval group in the **User group** column:
   - To assign an existing group, select it from the dropdown.
   - To create a new group, right-click the dropdown arrow, select **View details**, then click **New**.
9. Enter a name for the group and the **User group** name.
10. Switch to the **Users** tab, search for and select the approvers.
11. Click **Save** and return.
12. Select the new **User group** and click **Save**.
13. Select the scholarship or discount you created.
14. Go to the **General** tab and click **Items** under **Fees**.
15. Click **New** and select the relevant fees; repeat as needed.
16. Click **Save** and return.
17. Go to the **General** tab and click **Students**.
18. Click **New** and add the student.
19. Enter the discount amount under **Discount %**.
20. Enter the **Effective date** and **Expiration date**.
21. From the **Approval** dropdown, select **Report as ready**.
22. Repeat to fully approve by selecting **Approve**.
23. Click **Save**.

---

## Subject and event names setup

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** from the sidebar.
2. Select **Academic Management**.
3. Expand **Setup** and click **Subject and event names**.
4. Click **New** in the toolbar.
5. Enter or create a code in the **Code** column.
6. Enter the subject or event name.
7. Select the correct **Category** from the dropdown.
8. Check **Activate**.
9. Click **Save**.
10. Navigate to **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management** ▸ **Inquiries and reports** ▸ **Fee schedules**.
11. Click **Sessional class and event details**.
12. Click **New** in the toolbar.
13. Add the student under **Student account**.
14. Add the code for the sessional class or event.
15. Enter **Session** numbers if required (leave `0` for single sessions).
16. Enter the **Effective date** and **Expiration date**.
17. Click **Save**.

---

## Create fee schedule templates

### Fee invoice template setup
1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management** ▸ **Fee schedules** ▸ **All fee schedules**.
2. Click **New** in the toolbar.
3. Enter details in **Description** (e.g., `Fee invoice`).
4. Choose the **Billing interval**.
5. Click **+ Add line** to create the fee schedule.
6. Select the required fee item in **Item number**.
7. If needed, check **Condition** and define criteria.
8. Add discount lines as required:
   - Set **Quantity** to `-1`.
   - Set **Apply to** to `Scholarship and discount`.
9. Click **Save**.

### Event template setup
1. From **All fee schedules**, click **New**.
2. Enter details in **Description** (e.g., `Event template`).
3. Choose the **Billing interval**.
4. Click **+ Add line** and select the event item.
5. Set **Apply to** to `Session class and events`.
6. If session-based, check **Is session based**.
7. Add conditions if required.
8. Click **Save**.

# Create Fee Invoices

## What you’ll do
Start a batch to generate fee invoices for selected students using one or more fee schedule templates. You can run it interactively for small groups or as a background batch for large cohorts.

---

## Run the task (Generate fee invoices)

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Periodic tasks** and select **Generate sales order batch processing**.
3. In the dialog, set **Fee generation date** and **Posting date**.
4. Choose the **Posting option**:
   - **Create sale orders** — creates sales orders for manual review and posting.
   - **Post invoice automatically** — creates and posts invoices in one step.
5. Enter a description in **Description** (e.g., `Term 3 Fees — Year 10–12`).
6. Decide whether to **Consolidate by item**:
   - **Yes** — separates invoices by item.
   - **No** — generates one invoice per student for the entire template.
7. Use **Customer filter** to select specific students or groups (e.g., by academic year).
8. In **Records to include**, select one or more fee schedule templates.
9. Under **Run in the background**, turn on **Batch processing** for large student numbers to avoid performance issues.
10. Click **OK** to start the process.
11. The system will notify you when the task is processing and when it’s complete.

---

## Reconcile the generated sales orders

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Fee schedule batches** and select **All fee schedule batches**.
3. Click the relevant **Fee schedule batch number** to view details.
4. Open **Fee generation reconciliation report** to check:
   - How many fee payer accounts were included.
   - How many students were included.
5. To export data, right-click the **Fee payer name** column header and select **Export all rows**.
6. In the dialog, click **Download** to export to Excel or SSRS for further validation if needed.

---

## ✅ Tips for success

- **Performance:** Use **Batch processing** for large cohorts to keep the UI responsive.
- **Clarity:** Add a clear **Description** so batches are easy to find later.
- **Posting control:** Use **Create sale orders** if you want a manual review step before posting invoices.
- **Templates:** Double-check you’ve selected the correct fee schedule templates before running the batch.

# Post Fee Invoice and Settle Deposit

## What you’ll do
Post fee invoices for students by selecting the correct fee schedule batch, reviewing sales orders, and initiating posting. Then verify that enrolment deposits are automatically settled against posted invoices for accurate reconciliation.

---

## Post fee invoices

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Fee schedule batches** and click **All fee schedule batches**.
3. Select the **Fee schedule batch number** you want to post (status must be **Active**).
4. Click **Post** in the toolbar.
5. Choose the **Posting date** (e.g., end of June).
6. Decide whether to:
   - Post directly, or
   - Run as a **background job** (recommended for large batches).
7. If running in the background, submit and wait for processing.
8. When complete, the batch status changes from **Active** to **Invoice**, and invoices are generated.

---

## Settle enrolment deposits

1. From **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management** ▸ **Inquiries and reports** ▸ **Pre-admission fees**.
2. Click **Pre-admission deposits**.
3. Click **OK** in the dialog.
4. Review deposits with status **`received`** (indicating payment).
5. After posting, the system automatically matches these deposits to invoices.
6. Confirm the deposit status changes from **`received`** to **`settle`**.
