# Student Visa Management

## What you’ll do
Manage visa types and student visa details in the academic management system. This includes navigating visa types, creating new visa entries (if required), viewing visa details for individual students, and generating reports of students with visas.

---

## Visa Types navigation

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Setup** and click **Visa types**.

---

## Create new Visa type
*(New entries are typically pulled from CE, not manually created)*

1. Click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Enter the **Visa ID**.
3. Enter the **Visa Name**.
4. If the Visa type is chargeable, select the checkbox. Leave blank if not chargeable.
5. If the Visa type is active, select the checkbox. Leave blank if not active.
6. Click **Save**.

---

## Visa details on students

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Students** and click **All Students**.
3. Select or search for a student account and click the student whose details you want to view.
4. Scroll down to the **Other Information** tab to view Visa details.

---

## View all students’ Visa Types

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Enquiries and reports**, then expand **Visa Management**.
3. Click **Students with Visa**.
4. Use the checkbox to filter by status.
