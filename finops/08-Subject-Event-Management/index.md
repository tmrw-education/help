# Subject & Event Management

Subject & Event Management handles the setup and invoicing of fee-generating activities including excursions, music lessons, and other school events. Fee categories, subject and event codes, and event fee schedule templates are configured before students are enrolled into sessional classes or events. Invoicing can be set to charge a flat fee or calculated based on sessions attended. Where session numbers change after invoices have been generated, adjustment processing is available. Enrolment cancellations are also managed here.
