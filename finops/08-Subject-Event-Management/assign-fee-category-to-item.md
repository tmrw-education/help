# Assign Fee Category to an Item

1. Open the item

   From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules ▸ Product information management**, expand **Products**, and click **Released products**. Find and select the item (e.g., *Piano fee*) (③). Click **Edit** in the Action Pane (④).

   ![Assign Fee Category to an Item — Piano fee selected with Edit button highlighted](./images/assign-fee-category-to-item-1.png)

2. Set the fee category

   Expand the **Sell** section below (⑤) and set the **Fee category** (⑥).

   ![Assign Fee Category to an Item - the Released products Sell section](./images/assign-fee-category-to-item-2.png)

   ![Assign Fee Category to an Item - Fee category field in the Sell section](./images/assign-fee-category-to-item-3.png)

3. Save

   Click **Save**.
