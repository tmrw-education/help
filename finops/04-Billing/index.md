# Billing

Billing manages the end-to-end process of generating and posting fee invoices for students. It covers fee schedule template creation, running batch jobs to generate sales orders, posting invoices by term, handling pro rata adjustments for students who join or leave mid-term, split billing configuration, proforma invoice generation and distribution, advance tax invoice requests, fee structure pricing through trade agreements, and student ledger reporting.
