# View Student Ledger

The student ledger provides a consolidated view of a student's financial activity over a specified date range. Finance staff use it to review invoices, payments, advance invoices, and prepayments for an individual student.

1. Open the student record and generate the ledger

   From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules**, expand **Students**, and click **All Students**. Select the student (③) and click **Collect** (④) on the Action Pane. Click **Statements** (⑤) and complete the following:

   - **From date** — enter the start of the date range (⑥-⑦).
   - **To date** — enter the end of the date range (⑥-⑦).
   - **Suppress Advance Invoice** (⑧) — enable this toggle to exclude advance invoice and prepayment invoice lines, producing a simplified statement view.

   Click **OK** to generate the report.
