# Intercompany Transactions

The intercompany transfer process is used when a student moves from one school to another and holds a credit balance in the originating school's system. Finance staff transfer that credit balance to the destination school so it can be applied to the student's account there. Before a transfer can be processed, any advanced tax invoices must be cancelled, as the system only supports the transfer of net credit balances.
