# Debtor Management

Debtor Management covers the student and fee payer record management that underpins accurate fee generation. Before running any fee batch, verify that each student has the correct academic year, enrolment dates, and sibling order assigned, and that financial responsibility percentages across all fee payers on a student's account total 100. Any errors at the debtor level flow directly through to generated invoices.
