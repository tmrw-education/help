# Create a consent record for a student

Consent is captured against the student's profile in CE. A student can have several consent records of different types — for example consent to share information, consent to share achievements, or medical consent.

1. Open the student's profile

   In the CE model-driven app, open the student's profile and go to the **Consent** area.
