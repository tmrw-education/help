# Rescind a specific consent

Rescinding withdraws a single consent previously given for a student, leaving
their other consents in place.

1. Open the student's Consent area

   In the CE model-driven app, open the student's profile and go to the
   **Consent** area.

2. Open the consent record to withdraw

   Open the specific consent record you want to rescind.

   ![The Consent area with a single consent record open, ready to be rescinded](./images/rescind-a-specific-consent-1.png)

3. Rescind and save

   Rescind that consent and save.

   > **Note:** Rescinding affects one consent only. To withdraw everything for a
   > student, see [Rescind all consent](./rescind-all-consent.md).
