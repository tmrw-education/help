# Record vitals, measurements, and vision screening on a visit

Vitals are taken when the student arrives at the medical centre, before any
treatment is applied. Measurements and vision screening are captured in the same
part of the form, and are typically used during health screening rather than for
a walk-in visit.

> **Note:** Medical centre records are restricted. Creating a visit record is
> limited to medical centre staff; viewing is limited to medical centre staff and
> the principal.

1. Open the vitals section

   Open the **Medical centre visit** and go to the vitals section.

2. Record the vitals

   Capture:

   - **Blood pressure**
   - **Heart rate**
   - **Temperature**

   ![The vitals section showing blood pressure, heart rate and temperature](./images/record-vitals-measurements-and-vision-screening-on-a-visit-1.png)

3. Add measurements and vision screening

   Where you are screening rather than treating, record the student's
   measurements and vision screening values.

4. Save

   Save the visit before moving on to treatment.
