# Search student by name, ID, or barcode scan on visit form

Every visit starts by identifying the student. Getting the right student matters
more here than almost anywhere else in the system, because the record drives
medication decisions.

1. Open a new visit

   In the **Administration** app, open **Student medical** and create a new
   **Medical centre visit**.

2. Find the student

   Search for the student by name or by student ID.

   ![The student lookup on a new medical centre visit](./images/search-student-by-name-id-or-barcode-scan-on-visit-form-1.png)

3. Select and check

   Select the student and confirm the details shown match the person in front of
   you before recording anything.

   > **Note:** Check the student's medical detail — allergies in particular —
   > before administering any treatment or medication.
