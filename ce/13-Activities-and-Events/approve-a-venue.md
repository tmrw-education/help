# Approve a venue

A new venue has to be approved before it can be selected on an event.

1. Open the approval record

   Open the venue and go to its approval record. Approvers should also receive an
   email and a Teams notification linking them straight to it.

2. Review the venue

   Check the venue detail — whether it is internal or external, the school it
   belongs to, and its type.

   ![A venue approval record showing the approve and reject options](./images/approve-a-venue-1.png)

3. Approve

   Click **Approve**. Once a decision has been made the approve and reject
   buttons no longer appear on the record.

4. Confirm it is usable

   Open an event and check the venue can now be selected. Selecting it pulls the
   venue detail through onto the event.

   > **Note:** If approval requests are not reaching the right person, check the
   > approval matrix for that school and process type — see
   > [Configure approval for venue](../20-Activities-and-Events-Setup/configure-approval-for-venue.md).
