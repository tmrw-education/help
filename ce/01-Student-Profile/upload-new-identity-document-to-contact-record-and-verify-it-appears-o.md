# Upload new identity document to Contact record and verify it appears on linked Student Profile

Identity documents — passport, visa, ID card — are held against the **Contact**
record, because they are personal rather than academic. Uploading stores a
digital copy that can be opened for reference later.

1. Open the contact

   Open the student's **Contact** record and find the legal identification,
   passport and visa section.

2. Upload the document

   Select the document type and upload the file. The digital copy is stored in
   SharePoint against the contact.

   ![Uploading an identity document to a contact record](./images/upload-new-identity-document-to-contact-record-and-verify-it-appears-o-1.png)

3. Check you can open it

   Open the uploaded document and compare it against the values keyed into the
   record.

4. Check it appears on the profile

   Open the linked student profile and confirm the document is reachable from
   there.

   > **Note:** Parents upload their own documents through the Parent Experience
   > Platform when prompted that a document is expiring. Documents are not
   > uploaded by external practitioners.
