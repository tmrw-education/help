# Verify Medical Details are correctly displayed on Student Profile

The wellbeing section of the student profile surfaces the student's medical
picture — conditions, allergies, doctor details and medical consent — so staff
can see it without going into the medical module.

1. Open the wellbeing section

   Open the student profile and go to the wellbeing section.

2. Check the medical detail

   Confirm the conditions shown match the medical detail records — condition
   name, diagnosis, and whether a medication plan or care plan is required.

   ![The wellbeing section of a student profile showing medical details](./images/verify-medical-details-are-correctly-displayed-on-student-profile-1.png)

3. Check the allergies

   Allergies are held separately from conditions, with their own symptoms, doctor
   instruction and severity level. Confirm they are listed.

4. Check the doctor and consent detail

   Confirm the doctor details and medical consent — for example consent for
   paracetamol or emergency treatment — are shown.

   > **Note:** Access to medical records is restricted. Medical centre visit
   > records in particular are limited to medical centre staff and the principal,
   > so what you see here depends on your role.
