# Verify Wellbeing Notes subgrid displays correctly on Student Profile

Wellbeing notes raised about a student are listed on their profile, giving a
single view of concerns raised over time regardless of who raised them or where.

1. Open the wellbeing section

   Open the student profile and go to the wellbeing section.

2. Check the notes are listed

   Confirm the wellbeing notes subgrid lists the notes raised about this student.

   ![The wellbeing notes subgrid on a student profile](./images/verify-wellbeing-notes-subgrid-displays-correctly-on-student-profile-1.png)

3. Open a note

   Open one of the notes and confirm it resolves to the full wellbeing note
   record — see
   [View wellbeing note details](../04-Wellbeing/view-wellbeing-note-details.md).

   > **Note:** Notes raised in the Staff Experience Platform and notes raised
   > directly in CE both appear here.
