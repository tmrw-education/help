# Manual sync from Teams to CE

**Sync assignment** moves a single class's assignment data between Teams Edu and
CE. Run it twice in the normal cycle: once after the assignment is created, and
again after it has been marked and returned.

1. Create the assignment in Teams

   In the class Team, create the assignment. Set the instructions and the maximum
   number of points that can be awarded, then assign it to the class.

   ![An assignment being created in the class Team](./images/manual-sync-from-teams-to-ce-1.png)

2. Sync it into CE

   In CE, open the class and click **Sync assignment**. A teacher assessment
   record is created carrying the instructions, the max points, the assign date
   and the due date.

   ![The synced teacher assessment record in CE](./images/manual-sync-from-teams-to-ce-2.png)

3. Let students submit and mark the work

   Students turn work in through Teams. The teacher marks it and clicks
   **Return** to release the mark.

4. Sync again to bring the marks back

   Click **Sync assignment** again. The published points now appear in CE
   alongside the assigned and submitted dates.

   > **Note:** Marks only come back once the work has been *returned* in Teams.
   > Marking without returning leaves the assessment showing as submitted but
   > ungraded.
