# Create a Team on Teams Edu manually

Classes normally become Teams automatically at the start of the academic year.
Creating one manually is the exception — use it when a class is set up after the
bulk run, or when a Team needs to be recreated.

1. Open the class

   In the **Administrator** app, open **Curriculum Management** and go to
   **Classes**. Open the class you want to create a Team for.

2. Check the class is complete

   Confirm the **Key teacher**, the **Course**, the **Year group** and the
   **Roll group** are set. Add any **Additional teachers** who support the class,
   and check the student list.

   ![A class record showing key teacher, additional teachers and students](./images/create-a-team-on-teams-edu-manually-1.png)

   > **Note:** Students without an external ID — their Microsoft identity — are
   > removed from the sync automatically and will not appear in the Team. Check
   > the student list after syncing rather than assuming everyone came across.

3. Sync to Teams

   Click **Sync to Teams**. The integration calls Teams Edu directly, so it takes
   a few moments to complete.

4. Confirm in Teams

   Open Teams and check the Team has been created with the expected name, and
   that the key teacher and additional teachers appear as members.

   ![The created class Team in Teams Edu showing its members](./images/create-a-team-on-teams-edu-manually-2.png)

5. Check the link is stored

   Back on the class in CE, the Teams URL is stored against the record. This is
   what later assignment syncs use.
