# Muster Point Coordinator records note and manages roll groups

The muster point coordinator sits between the teachers at their assembly point and
the emergency coordinator running the incident, making sure every roll group
assigned to that point is accounted for.

1. Open your muster point

   Emergency attendance records carry a **Muster point**. Open the records for
   the muster point you are coordinating.

2. Work through the roll groups

   Each year group has a muster point, and each roll group reporting to it has its
   own emergency attendance record. Check each one has been completed by the
   teacher.

   ![Emergency attendance records grouped by muster point](./images/muster-point-coordinator-records-note-and-manages-roll-groups-1.png)

3. Add notes

   Add attendance notes to pass information up to the emergency coordinator —
   students still missing, students moved to another point, anything the
   coordinator needs to know.

4. Report complete

   Once every roll group at your point is accounted for, confirm to the emergency
   coordinator.
