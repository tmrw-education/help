# Teacher records emergency note and marks student attendance status

When an emergency is declared, the teacher who was with the class becomes
responsible for accounting for those students at the muster point.

1. Open your emergency attendance

   Emergency attendance records are created against the teacher who was teaching
   the class at the time the emergency was declared. Open the record for your
   class.

2. Mark each student

   Set each student's status:

   - **Found** — the student is with you at the muster point
   - **Missing** — the student is not accounted for
   - **Absent** — the student was not in school

   ![An emergency attendance record with student statuses being set](./images/teacher-records-emergency-note-and-marks-student-attendance-status-1.png)

   > **Note:** Students already marked absent in normal attendance carry through
   > as absent here, so the coordinator sees one consolidated picture rather than
   > having to cross-check two rolls.

3. Add a note

   Add an attendance note where something needs explaining — a student sent to
   the medical centre, or last seen heading in a particular direction. Notes are
   how teachers, muster point coordinators and the emergency coordinator pass
   information to each other.

4. Confirm

   Confirm the record once every student on your list is accounted for.
