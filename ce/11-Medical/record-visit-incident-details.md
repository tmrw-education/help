# Record Visit & Incident details

The incident section of the visit captures what happened, where, and what the
student presented with — the detail that supports any follow-up or reporting.

> **Note:** Medical centre records are restricted. Creating a visit record is
> limited to medical centre staff; viewing is limited to medical centre staff and
> the principal.

1. Open the visit and select the student

   Create or open the **Medical centre visit** and select the student.

2. Set the reason for visit

   Choose the **Reason for visit** from the list — for example Physical injury.

   ![The reason for visit selected on a medical centre visit](./images/record-visit-incident-details-1.png)

3. Record the incident detail

   Capture where the incident happened, the injured body area, the cause, and the
   symptoms presented. These are all chosen from standing lists so that reporting
   stays consistent between staff.

4. Save

   Save the visit and continue to the vitals and treatment.
