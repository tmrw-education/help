# Record treatment

Treatment records what was actually done for the student during the visit. It
sits between the vitals and the outcome.

> **Note:** Medical centre records are restricted. Creating a visit record is
> limited to medical centre staff; viewing is limited to medical centre staff and
> the principal.

1. Open the treatment section

   Open the **Medical centre visit** and go to the treatment section.

2. Record what was applied

   Select the treatment given — for example first aid or wound care.

3. Add the nurse note

   Write the note describing what was done. This is the clinical record of the
   visit, so be specific.

   ![The treatment section with a nurse note recorded](./images/record-treatment-1.png)

4. Save

   Save the visit. Add medication separately if any was given — see
   [Log medication administration](./log-medication-administration.md).
