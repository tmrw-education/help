# Complete end-to-end visit

This is the full path through a medical centre visit, from a student arriving at
the clinic to the parent being told what happened.

> **Note:** Medical centre records are restricted. Creating a visit record is
> limited to medical centre staff; viewing is limited to medical centre staff and
> the principal.

1. Create the visit and identify the student

   In the **Administration** app, open **Student medical** and create a new
   **Medical centre visit**. Search for and select the student.

2. Record why they came

   Set the **Reason for visit** — for example Physical injury.

3. Take the vitals

   Record blood pressure, heart rate and temperature.

   ![The vitals section of a medical centre visit](./images/complete-end-to-end-visit-1.png)

4. Record the treatment

   Add the treatment applied — for example first aid or wound care — with a
   nurse note describing what was done. Save.

5. Add medication if given

   Where medication was administered, add it to the visit with the medicine
   type, dose, unit, frequency and instruction. See
   [Log medication administration](./log-medication-administration.md).

6. Set the outcome

   Choose the **Final outcome type**:

   - **Return to class** — the student is well enough to go back
   - **Send home** — the parent collects them
   - **Hospital transfer** — the student is moved on for further care

   Add the **Final outcome note**.

7. Notify the parent

   Turn on the parent notification and write the message the parent will
   receive. Save.

   ![The final outcome and parent notification on a visit](./images/complete-end-to-end-visit-2.png)

   > **Note:** For Send home, create a gate pass as well so the student is
   > authorised to leave the site.
