# Navigate Medical Centre Visit form

The medical centre visit record captures a single student visit from arrival
through to outcome. The form runs in the order you would actually work: who came
in, why, what you measured, what you did, and what happened next.

> **Note:** Medical centre records are restricted. Creating a visit record is
> limited to medical centre staff; viewing is limited to medical centre staff and
> the principal.

1. Open the visit record

   In the **Administration** app, open **Student medical** and create a new
   **Medical centre visit**.

   ![The Student medical area with Medical centre visit selected](./images/navigate-medical-centre-visit-form-1.png)

2. Identify the student

   Search for and select the student.

3. Record why they came

   Set the **Reason for visit** — for example Physical injury.

4. Record the vitals

   Capture blood pressure, heart rate and temperature.

5. Record the treatment

   Add the treatment applied and the accompanying nurse note.

6. Close out the visit

   Set the **Final outcome type** — Return to class, Send home or Hospital
   transfer — add the **Final outcome note**, and notify the parent with a
   message where needed.

   ![A completed medical centre visit showing the final outcome section](./images/navigate-medical-centre-visit-form-2.png)

   > **Note:** Where the outcome is Send home, the medical centre can also create
   > a gate pass so the student can leave and the parent knows to collect them.
