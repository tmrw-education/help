# View and filter medication administration history

The medication history shows every dose recorded for a student, so you can check
what has already been given before administering anything further.

> **Note:** Medical centre records are restricted. Creating a visit record is
> limited to medical centre staff; viewing is limited to medical centre staff and
> the principal.

1. Open the history

   Open the student's medical record and go to the medication history.

2. Filter it

   Filter by date range or by medicine type to narrow the list.

3. Open a record

   Open an individual record to see the dose, unit, frequency, instruction and
   the course start and end dates.

   ![The medication administration history for a student](./images/view-and-filter-medication-administration-history-1.png)

   > **Note:** Always check the history before administering. The course end date
   > matters — a record still on file does not mean the course is current.
