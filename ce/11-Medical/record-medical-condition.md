# Record Medical Condition

Medical details hold the conditions a school needs to know about for a student.
Most arrive with the enrolment application, but they can be added or updated here
as circumstances change.

> **Note:** Medical centre records are restricted. Creating a visit record is
> limited to medical centre staff; viewing is limited to medical centre staff and
> the principal.

1. Open Medical detail

   Open the student's **Medical detail** and create a new record.

2. Record the condition

   Enter the **Condition name** and the **Diagnosis** given by the doctor or
   general practitioner.

   ![A medical detail record showing condition name and diagnosis](./images/record-medical-condition-1.png)

3. Set the plans

   Turn on **Is medication required** if the condition needs a medication plan,
   and **Care plan required** if it needs a care plan. Turning either on reveals
   the fields for that plan — see
   [Toggle Medication Plan fields](./toggle-medication-plan-fields-based-on-is-medication-required.md)
   and [Toggle Care Plan fields](./toggle-care-plan-fields-based-on-care-plan-required.md).

4. Attach supporting documents

   Upload any supporting documentation. Documents are stored in SharePoint
   against the record.

5. Save

   Save the medical detail.

   > **Note:** Allergies are held separately from medical conditions, with their
   > own symptoms, doctor instruction and severity level.
