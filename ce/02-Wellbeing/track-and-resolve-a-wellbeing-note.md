# Track and Resolve a wellbeing note

Resolving the case is the final step. When the case is resolved, the wellbeing
note that opened it becomes inactive automatically, and the change is reflected
back to the Staff Experience Platform.

1. Check the case is ready

   Open the wellbeing case and confirm the associated tasks are closed and the
   timeline records what was done.

2. Hand the case over if needed

   If the case belongs with someone else, change the **Owner** to that staff
   member and save. They will then see the case and can resolve it themselves.

   ![Changing the owner on a wellbeing case](./images/track-and-resolve-a-wellbeing-note-1.png)

3. Resolve

   On the case form, click **Resolve Case**. Set the **Resolution type** — for
   example Completed — and write the **Resolution**. Click **Resolve**.

   ![The Resolve Case dialog with resolution type and resolution note](./images/track-and-resolve-a-wellbeing-note-2.png)

4. Confirm the note closed

   Go back to the wellbeing note and refresh. Its status is now **Inactive**.

   > **Note:** The note goes inactive on its own — you do not close it
   > separately. If it is still active, the case has not resolved successfully.
