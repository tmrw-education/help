# Verify parent relationship management — Parent 1 and Parent 2 in single view

Confirm that a parent profile presents Parent 1 and Parent 2 together in a single
view, with the relationship fields populated for each.

1. Open the parent profile

   From the **App selector**, open the **Administrator** app, go to **General →
   Parent profile**, and **Open** the record you want to check.

   ![The parent profile open in the Administrator app](./images/verify-parent-relationship-management-1.png)

2. Check both parents appear together

   Confirm Parent 1 and Parent 2 are shown in the same view rather than on
   separate records, and that the relationship fields are populated for each.

   > **Note:** A parent is a **Contact** record extended into a parent profile.
   > Personal detail belongs to the contact; the relationship to the student
   > belongs to the profile.
