# Assign Emergency Coordinator and Muster Point Coordinator roles

Two roles run an emergency. The **emergency coordinator** declares the emergency
for the school, monitors progress and stands the school down. **Muster point
coordinators** account for the roll groups assembling at their point and report
up.

1. Assign the emergency coordinator

   Identify who will act as emergency coordinator for the school. This person
   creates the emergency case, which is what switches the school into emergency
   mode — see [Initiate Emergency](../07-Emergency-Attendance/initiate-emergency.md).

2. Assign muster point coordinators

   Assign a coordinator to each muster point. They are responsible for the roll
   groups reporting to that point.

3. Check access before you need it

   Confirm both roles can reach what they need — the emergency coordinator needs
   to be able to create and resolve the emergency case, and coordinators need to
   see the emergency attendance records for their point.

   ![Emergency roles assigned ahead of an emergency](./images/assign-emergency-coordinator-and-muster-point-coordinator-roles-1.png)

   > **Note:** Test this outside an emergency. Finding out someone cannot open
   > the record during an actual incident is too late.
