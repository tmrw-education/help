# Reject a venue

Reject a venue where it is not suitable — for example an external venue that has
not passed a risk assessment.

1. Open the approval record

   Open the venue and go to its approval record.

2. Review the venue

   Check the venue detail before deciding.

3. Reject

   Click **Reject**. The venue is not made available for selection on events.

   ![A venue approval record being rejected](./images/reject-a-venue-1.png)

   > **Note:** Once rejected, the approve and reject buttons disappear from the
   > record. Raising the venue again means creating a new record.
