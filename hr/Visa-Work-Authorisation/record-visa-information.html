Record visa information for an employee

The visa information form captures all fields required for visa processing and regulatory compliance for an individual employee. This is typically completed by the HR or PRO team.

  1. Open the employee record

    In D365, navigate to the relevant employee record within the Human Resources module.

  2. Open Visa Information

    Within the employee record, locate and open the Visa Information section.

  3. Create a new record

    Click New to start a new visa entry.

  4. Complete all required fields

    Fill in all applicable fields. Dropdown lists show profession descriptions alongside codes for clarity when making selections:

    • UID Number
    • Visa Type
    • Visa Programme
    • MOL Profession
    • MOE Profession
    • Working Unit
    • Labour Card details
    • ABC Category
    • Contract Type

  5. Enter dates

    Enter the issue date and expiry date for the visa or labour card.

  6. Save the record

    Click Save to record the information.

    Once saved, expiry dates are monitored by the system. Alerts appear in the employee's ESS dashboard tiles and in the notification bell as expiry approaches, based on the threshold configured per document type.
