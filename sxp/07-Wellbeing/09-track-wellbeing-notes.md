# Track my notes

**Track wellbeing notes** is your personal workspace for the open tasks assigned
to you and the wellbeing notes you own, split across three tabs.

1. Open your tracking workspace

   On the **Wellbeing** landing page, click **Track my notes** at the top right.

   {/* CAPTURE: Wellbeing landing page with the Track my notes button at the top right */}

2. Check your open tasks

   On the **Open tasks** tab, see the tasks assigned to you across every case
   — Task title, Case, Student, Priority. This tab defaults to showing **Open**
   tasks; clear or change the **status** filter to see completed and cancelled
   ones. **Search**, filter by **status** and **priority**, and click a row to
   **expand** it for the description, status, created/updated dates, and
   **Open case** / **Edit task** buttons.

   {/* CAPTURE: Open tasks tab with one row expanded showing Open case and Edit task buttons */}

3. Review your active notes

   On the **Active notes** tab, see the wellbeing notes you currently own, in
   the same table layout as the landing page.

4. Look back at closed notes

   The **Closed/resolved notes** tab lists your resolved and cancelled notes.

5. Open a case

   Click a **Case** link (in tasks) or a **Case title** (in notes) to open that
   case's detail page. The count in each tab label reflects what that tab will
   load, so you can size up your workload before opening it.
