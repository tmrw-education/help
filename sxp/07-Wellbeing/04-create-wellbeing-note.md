# Create a wellbeing note

A wellbeing note records a concern, an observation, or a positive note about a
student, and opens a case you can track from there. Creating a note needs
**Wellbeing write** access (pastoral and student-support staff, leaders);
read-only staff see a disabled **Create wellbeing note** button, which is also
disabled when you are offline.

1. Open the form

   On the **Wellbeing** landing page, click **Create wellbeing note** (top
   right). It opens as a single **Create wellbeing note** window with **Subject**,
   **Note**, **Link**, **Notifications**, and **Attachments** sections.

   {/* CAPTURE: the Create wellbeing note modal open on the Wellbeing landing page, showing the Subject and Note sections */}

2. Choose the student

   In **Subject**, search for and pick the **Student name**. Their **Student ID**
   and **Year group** fill in automatically and are read-only.

   > **Note:** The student is fixed once you publish. You cannot reassign a note
   > to a different student later, so pick the right one now.

3. Write the note

   In **Note**, enter the **Title** (up to 100 characters), choose a **Note
   type**, write the **Description**, and set a **Sentiment** (which drives the
   metrics cards on the Wellbeing landing page) and a **Priority** (Low, Medium,
   High, or Critical). All of these are required.

4. Add links, notifications, or attachments

   Optionally, in **Link** attach a related **Linked note**; in **Notifications**
   pick staff to notify (adding any staff member switches staff notification on;
   leaving it empty leaves it off) and set **Notify parents**; in **Attachments**
   upload photos or documents (max 10 MB each: .jpg, .png, .pdf, .doc, .docx).

   > **Note:** **Notify parents** defaults to On. Turn it off before publishing
   > if parents should not be notified.

5. Publish

   Click **Publish note**, which stays disabled until every required field is
   valid. A "Wellbeing note created" toast confirms it.
