Use your Attendance page

Quick answer

  • Open Attendance from the navigation to land on your teacher page.
  • Three stat cards sit above your day's calendar.
  • Click a class on the calendar to open its roll.
  • Each card's Show top 10 opens a list of those students.

The Attendance page is your teacher landing screen: three stat cards on the left and a calendar of your day's classes below. The same Attendance nav item opens a whole-school dashboard instead if you're a school leader or attendance officer.

  1. Open Attendance

    Click Attendance in the main navigation. As a regular teacher you land on the stat cards and your day's calendar.

  2. Read the three stat cards

    The cards summarise today across all your classes: Late students and Unexcused absences (both for today), and Absent students (this month, on assessment days). Each card shows a count with a "from yesterday" or "from previous month" change.

  3. See who's in a card

    Click a card's Show top 10 to open a list of those students.

  4. Open a class roll

    The calendar shows your classes for the day. Click a class to open its roll and start marking students.

  5. View analytics

    Click View analytics (top right) to open Attendance analytics. The link is only enabled if you have attendance-analytics access.
