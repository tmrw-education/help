Glossary

Terms used across StaffXP and this guide.

A · B · C · D · E · F · K · L · M · N · O · P · R · S · T · U · V · W · Y

A​

TermDefinition
Alert IconsThe row of four coloured icons over a student's photo — always Custody, Physical, Learning, Medical in that order. Full colour means the alert is active; muted means it isn't on file. Active icons open a pop-up; muted ones aren't clickable.
All ActivitiesAn Activities overview tab listing every approved activity org-wide. Only shows for activity coordinators and super admins.
All RequestsThe request queue coordinators and school leaders see instead of Your requests. In extra-curricular it opens pre-filtered to Pending as a triage queue.
Approved AbsenceA resolved absence set by an attendance officer. Locked to teachers — clicking it opens the absence's details — and skipped by bulk marking so it's never overwritten.
At a GlanceThe landing tab of a student profile: photo, core identity details, the alert icon row, shared documents, and the alert key.
Attendance AnalyticsThe screen holding the at-risk banner, four stat cards, the Trends charts, and the at-risk table. Needs Attendance-analytics read access.
Attendance OverviewThe whole-school attendance dashboard that school leaders and attendance officers land on. Holds the Roll reminders, Students at risk and View trends tiles, three charts, and the Class rolls list.
Attendance RollThe table listing every student in a class on one page — no pagination, so nobody is missed. Shows up to four prior sessions read-only on the left and today's editable column on the right.
Authorised ContactsThe people cleared to collect a student, listed on the Caregivers tab with name, address and phone number.

B​

TermDefinition
Booking StatusA read-only tab on the Activities and Extra-curricular hubs listing resource bookings — Event, Resource, Quantity, Picked up by, Return date and Status. Resources are booked from an activity request's Resource booking step, not here.

C​

TermDefinition
CaregiversThe student profile tab holding Parent 1, Parent 2, Custody arrangements, Emergency contacts and Authorised contacts. Where the Custody alert sends you.
Case StatusThe state of a wellbeing case: Active (blue, open and being worked), Resolved (green, finished and read-only), or Cancelled (grey, closed without resolution and read-only).
Class RollsThe list on the Attendance overview from which leaders and officers open any class's roll across the school. Searchable by class or teacher, filterable by year group, with a date-range trigger for past sessions.
Cleared ClassesThe table on the Emergency coordinator page listing classes fully accounted for, so attention goes to the ones still outstanding.
Correction RequestA teacher's request to change an already-submitted attendance mark, logged through Felix and resolved by an attendance officer from their Correction request tab. The only way to change a locked mark.
Custody AlertA student alert indicating a custody order limits who may collect them. Opens the Caregivers tab so authorisation can be confirmed before release.

D​

TermDefinition
Do Not DisturbA toggle in the bell panel header that stops on-screen pop-up toasts. Notifications still arrive in the panel and the bell badge still updates. Remembered between sessions.
DraftA saved but unsubmitted request. Only New / Draft requests offer Edit and Delete; Save and exit creates one, and enables once the request has a name.

E​

TermDefinition
Emergency CoordinatorThe live page shown to emergency coordinators and school leaders during an active emergency. Holds the muster table of all classes and muster points, plus the Cleared classes table. Also where a leader ends the incident.
Emergency RollThe stripped-down headcount every teacher's roll becomes when a leader declares an emergency. Two columns only — Student name and Emergency roll — marked Present or Missing, with no all-marked gate on submit.
Enable WaitlistThe setting that lets students beyond the maximum join a waitlist rather than being turned away. Available on overseas trips and extra-curricular activities.
Exclude StudentsA review-only control in the Attendees section of an extra-curricular review screen. Students excluded there aren't enrolled when the request is approved.
Extra CurricularThe hub for the school's clubs and teams, holding the request queue, Booking status, Supervising activities and Staffing tabs.

F​

TermDefinition
FeaturedThe tab at the top of the Notices board holding starred notices. Featuring is a school leader action, applies to Active or Scheduled notices only, and has a daily cap.
FelixThe in-app assistant. Used to open a class roll, log an attendance correction request, and answer questions across the platform.

K​

TermDefinition
Key ContactsThe risk-management section of an activity or venue request holding the lead teacher, principal, deputy, first-aid teacher and the local emergency number. The emergency number is required for excursions, camps, overseas trips and extra-curricular activities, and must be a valid phone number.

L​

TermDefinition
Learning AlertA student alert indicating a diagnosed learning-support need. Links to the IEP and learning-disability documents.
Leave RequestsAn attendance officer tab on the Attendance overview holding parent-submitted leave to approve or decline.

M​

TermDefinition
Manage NoticesThe table of notices you've created, reached from the Notices board. Seeing it means you have editing access; school leaders also see everyone's notices.
Medical AlertA student alert indicating an active medical plan — allergies, medication, or condition protocols. Points to the Medical tab and Wellbeing tab.
MessagesThe notifications tab holding items addressed to you that usually carry something to do — pending requests, attendance summaries, approvals, wellbeing follow-ups. Clicking one opens what it's about.
More InfoA request status meaning an approver has sent it back with notes for you to update and resubmit. Read the notes via View review notes on the request row.
Muster CoordinatorThe live page shown to a muster-point coordinator during an emergency, covering just their own point's headcount and cleared classes.
MyDayThe StaffXP home page, reached by clicking StaffXP at the far left of the nav bar. Tiles show today's schedule, assignments to mark, the to-do list, notices, birthdays and open wellbeing tasks. Its look shifts through the day.

N​

TermDefinition
Not on RollThe group below an emergency roll holding students added from other classes. Each is marked Present and counts toward your Present total, with Remove and Edit/add note actions.
Not Yet MarkedThe default attendance status (shown as ···) before you mark a student. Blocks Submit until cleared.
Note TypeA required classification on a wellbeing note, set alongside sentiment and priority.
Notify ParentsA wellbeing note setting that defaults to On. Turn it off before publishing if parents shouldn't be notified.

O​

TermDefinition
Open TasksThe tab in Track my notes holding wellbeing tasks assigned to you across every case. Defaults to Open; change the status filter for completed and cancelled.

P​

TermDefinition
Parent VolunteersThe Activities overview tab holding parent helper sign-ups. Opens filtered to Pending / More info. Approving and declining is approver-only.
PendingA request status meaning it's been submitted and is awaiting a coordinator's decision.
Physical AlertA student alert indicating a recorded physical accommodation — mobility, sensory, or assistive equipment.
PriorityThe urgency set on a wellbeing note: Low, Medium, High or Critical.
Push NotificationsThe small pop-up cards (toasts) that slide into the corner when a notification arrives. On by default. Only appear when push is on and Do not disturb is off.

R​

TermDefinition
ReplicateThe row action on an Approved request that opens a pre-filled copy as a brand-new request. Dates and times — and for extra-curricular, the whole registration window — are cleared, so fresh ones must be entered. The original is untouched. Called Duplicate from the All activities tab.
Resource BookingThe optional second step of an activity request, covering Finance, Equipment, Transportation and Technology. Skippable entirely, but a started booking row must be finished — a category and a resource — or Next stays disabled.
Review NotesThe notes an approver must enter when declining a request or sending it back for more info. Read by the requester via View review notes on the request row.
Risk ManagementThe step in an activity, venue or extra-curricular request where each category — Student lists, Activity based, Transport, Staff capacity — either lists risks with mitigations or is explicitly marked "no risk". Visits have no Transport category.
Roll RemindersAn Attendance overview tile showing a live count of classes that haven't submitted their roll yet. View all lists them by teacher, class, period and outstanding count.

S​

TermDefinition
SentimentA required setting on a wellbeing note recording the tone of the observation.
Staff to NotifyThe escalation contacts listed for a student on the Wellbeing tab of their profile.
StaffingThe Extra-curricular tab listing staff assigned to activities with Name, Role, Phone, Email and Consent. Consent shows View for an external member's clearance document, Internal for internal staff, or a dash.
Status PillThe interactive control in a wellbeing case header used to move the case between Active, Resolved and Cancelled.
Students at RiskAn Attendance overview tile showing a live count of students below the attendance threshold.
Students Not on RollThe button on an emergency roll used to add a student from another class who is physically with you. Shows a live count once anyone is added; the same student can't be added twice.
Supervising ActivitiesThe Extra-curricular tab ("All ECAs") listing only Approved and Rejected activities. Approved rows offer Replicate; Rejected rows offer View review notes.

T​

TermDefinition
Today · All ClassesThe cross-class timeline in an expanded roll row showing every lesson a student has today, with your period ringed — so you can spot a student present in your class but absent later.
Track My NotesYour personal wellbeing workspace, reached from the Wellbeing landing page. Three tabs: Open tasks, Active notes, and Closed/resolved notes.
Turn Off Emergency ProtocolThe leader-only control that ends an emergency and returns every class to its normal roll. Sits in the top app toolbar and on the Emergency coordinator page. Needs the emergency deactivate permission.

U​

TermDefinition
Unresolved AttendanceAn attendance officer tab holding marks that still need a resolution category.
Unresolved LateAn attendance status a teacher can set, pending an officer assigning a specific reason.

V​

TermDefinition
Venue ConflictA clash between a requested venue and another approved event. Disables Approve on the review screen and raises a Venue conflict detected pop-up naming the conflicting event.
View AnalyticsThe button on the teacher Attendance page opening Attendance analytics. Stays disabled without Attendance-analytics read access.
View Review NotesThe row action on a Rejected / Declined or More info request that opens the approver's notes explaining the decision.
View TrendsThe tile on the leader / officer Attendance overview opening Attendance trends — absence timing, tardiness timing, and learning-area attendance bar lists.
VisibilityThe dates that control when a notice is shown on the board. Combined with audience type, this is why your board won't match a colleague's.

W​

TermDefinition
WaitlistThe table on an approved extra-curricular activity holding students beyond the maximum. Only appears when the activity has a waitlist enabled. Tick rows to reveal the batch Approve and Remove actions — there are no per-row buttons.
Wellbeing NoteA record of a concern, observation or positive note about a student, which opens a case you can track. Needs Wellbeing write access to create. The student is fixed once published and can't be reassigned.

Y​

TermDefinition
Your RequestsThe request queue general staff see on the Activities and Extra-curricular hubs, holding only their own submissions. Always shows all statuses.
