# View a student's parents, caregivers and emergency contacts

The **Caregivers** tab on a student's profile shows who the family is, how to
reach them, custody status, and who's authorised to collect or be contacted —
all read-only.

1. Open the Caregivers tab

   Go to the student's profile and select the **Caregivers** tab. The sections
   read top to bottom: **Parent 1**, **Parent 2**, **Custody arrangements**,
   **Emergency contacts** and **Authorised contacts**.

   {/* CAPTURE: a student profile with the Caregivers tab selected, showing the Parent 1 contact details and the contacts tables below */}

2. Read the parent contact details

   Each parent block lists **First name**, **Last name**, **Parent preferred
   name**, **Relationship to student**, **Home address**, the **Same as
   residential address** tick, **Mailing address**, **Parent's email**,
   **Communication preference**, **Phone number**, **Work** and **WhatsApp**, plus
   the parent's **Visa and passport details**. **Parent 2** appears only when a
   second parent is recorded, and a **Mailing address** is shown separately only
   when it differs from the home address.

3. Copy a value to your clipboard

   Home address, mailing address, email and phone numbers each have a **copy**
   button next to them — click it to copy the value, handy for dialling or pasting
   into an email. The copy button shows only when there's actually a value.

4. Check custody before releasing a student

   **Custody arrangements** is a single read-only tick: **Custody arrangements in
   place**. This tab is where the **Custody alert** sends you, so check it before
   you release a student or contact family.

   > **Note:** When custody arrangements are in place, confirm who is authorised
   > to collect the student here before letting them leave with anyone.

5. See emergency and authorised contacts

   **Emergency contacts** is a table of **Contact full name**, **Emergency
   ranking**, **Address** and **Phone number** — the ranking tells you who to call
   first. **Authorised contacts** lists the people cleared to collect the student,
   with **Contact full name**, **Address** and **Phone number**.
