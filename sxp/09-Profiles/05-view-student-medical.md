# View a student's medical information

The **Medical** tab gathers a student's condition, medication, care and medical
plans, vaccination record, and their doctor. Like the rest of the profile it is
read-only — you view and copy this information, you don't edit it here. This is
also where the **Medical alert** pop-up sends you for condition and medication
detail.

1. Open the Medical tab

   On the student's profile, click the **Medical** tab.

   {/* CAPTURE: a student profile with the Medical tab selected */}

2. Read Medical details

   The **Medical details** section lists the medical condition name, **Severity**,
   **Medication required** (Yes or No), and the **Start date** and **End date** of
   the condition.

3. Open the attached plans

   Below the details, **View care plan**, **View medication plan** and **View
   vaccination record** open the documents on file. Each link only appears when
   that document exists; otherwise the section shows a "nothing attached" line in
   its place. **View vaccination record** opens a pop-up with the latest record —
   student info, the **Is vaccinated** and **Parent consent** ticks, vaccination
   and administered dates, and links to the vaccination card and card URL.

   {/* CAPTURE: the View vaccination record pop-up showing the latest record */}

4. Read Doctor details

   The **Doctor details** section shows the doctor name, plus the medical centre
   name, address and phone — the contacts to call about this student's care.
