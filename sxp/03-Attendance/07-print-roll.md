# Print a class roll

When you need a paper copy of a class roll — for a substitute, an excursion, or
a fire-drill backup — you can print it straight from the roll.

1. Open the roll you want to print

   Open the class roll from your calendar, a roll reminder, or Felix.

2. Print it

   On the roll toolbar, click the **print** icon. The attendance document is
   fetched and sent to your printer.

   Printing needs a live connection — if you're offline it fails with a message,
   so reconnect and try again.
