# Add a student who isn't on your roll

When an emergency is declared, you take a stripped-down **Emergency roll** for
your class. If a student who belongs to another class is physically with you,
add them so they're accounted for in the headcount.

1. Open the off-roll list

   On the **Emergency roll**, click **Students not on roll**. The button shows a
   live count once you've added anyone.

   {/* CAPTURE: emergency roll header with the Students not on roll button */}

2. Add the student

   In the modal, click **Add**, then search and pick the student. You can add an
   optional **reason** of up to 100 characters. The same student can't be added
   twice — try it and you'll get an **Already added** warning.

   {/* CAPTURE: Students not on roll modal with the search picker and reason field */}

3. Check the off-roll group

   Added students appear in a **Not on roll** group below your roll, each marked
   **Present** and counting toward your **Present** total. Every row has a
   **Remove** action and an **Edit/add note** action.

4. Remove one if needed

   Click **Remove** on a row to take a student back off the list. A brief
   **Undo** appears if you remove someone by mistake.

5. Submit the roll

   When everyone with you is accounted for, click **Submit** to send your class's
   emergency status, including the off-roll students.
