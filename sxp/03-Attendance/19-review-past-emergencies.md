# Review past emergencies

After a drill or real incident ends, you can review what happened and how long
each part of the muster took. This is **leader-only**: the **Emergency**
history is reachable by school leaders, not teachers or attendance officers.

1. Open Emergency analytics

   On the **Attendance** page, open **Emergency**. This shows a history table of
   past incidents — both drills and real emergencies.

   {/* CAPTURE: the Emergency analytics history table of past incidents */}

2. Read the history table

   Each row is one past incident, with timing stats such as average clear time.
   Use the filters to narrow the list **by type**, **by term**, and **by year**.

3. Open one incident's full report

   Click an incident row to open **Emergency details** for that incident. It
   breaks down **time taken** per class and per muster point, and compares
   present against absent numbers.

   {/* CAPTURE: the Emergency details page for a single incident showing per-class and per-muster-point timings */}

4. Print the report

   The details page is printable, so you can keep a record of the incident or
   share it for a debrief.
