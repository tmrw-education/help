# End an emergency

When the evacuation or drill is over, a leader ends the emergency to take every
class out of emergency mode and return the school to normal attendance.

> **Note:** Ending an emergency is leader-only — it needs the *emergency deactivate*
> permission. Coordinators can see the live muster data but can't stand the incident
> down, so if the button isn't available to you, ask a leader to end it.

1. Click Turn off emergency protocol

   With an emergency active, the **Turn off emergency protocol** button sits in the
   top app toolbar and on the **Emergency coordinator** page. Click it from either
   place.

   {/* CAPTURE: Emergency coordinator page showing the Turn off emergency protocol button */}

2. Confirm in the dialog

   The **Turn off emergency protocol** dialog opens. Confirm to end the emergency
   for the whole school.

3. Check the confirmation

   A confirmation toast with a timestamp tells you the emergency has ended. Every
   class returns to its normal roll, and the emergency-coordinator and
   muster-coordinator pages close.
