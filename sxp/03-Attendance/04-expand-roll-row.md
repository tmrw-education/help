# See a student's day at a glance

Each row on the roll opens a quick-glance card that shows the student's whole
day across every class — not just your lesson.

1. Open the row

   On the roll, click the **expand chevron** at the start of a student's row.
   The card loads its detail the moment you open it, so it may take a beat to
   appear.

   {/* CAPTURE: an attendance roll with one student row expanded into its quick-glance card */}

2. Check any alerts

   At the top of the card, **alert icons** flag safeguarding, medical, learning
   or custody concerns. Click an icon to read its detail.

3. Read the student context

   The card shows the student's **preferred name**, **house**, **first
   language** and **age** where the school holds them.

4. See their whole day

   **Today · all classes** is a timeline of every lesson the student has today,
   with **your** period ringed and a one-line summary such as "2 present · 1
   late today". Because it's cross-class, it shows lessons you don't teach — so
   you can spot a student who's present in your class but absent later in the
   day.

5. Glance at their rate

   An **Attendance rate** big number sits on the right of the card.
