# StaffXP overview

StaffXP is your day-to-day hub for school life — attendance, notices, wellbeing,
activities, and more — all in one place. This guide is organised by feature; here's
the map.

## What's in StaffXP

- **[Navigating the platform](./02-Navigating/01-sign-in.md)** — sign in, find your
  way around the nav bar, your MyDay home page, themes, and settings.
- **[Attendance](./03-Attendance.md)** — track attendance for a single school day
  or across a week, for your classes or (for admins) the whole school.
- **[Activities](./05-Activities.md)** — plan school activities and manage
  resources against a shared calendar, so bookings don't clash.
- **[Extra-curricular](./06-Extracurricular.md)** — plan, browse, and create
  extra-curricular activities, and track them on a calendar.
- **[Wellbeing](./07-Wellbeing.md)** — monitor and support student wellbeing by
  creating and tracking wellbeing notes, with trends across categories.
- **[Notices](./Notices/01-find-and-read-notices.md)** — read the school
  noticeboard, follow the categories you care about, RSVP to events, and (with
  access) create and manage notices.
- **[Notifications](./08-Notifications.md)** — your messages and alerts.
- **[Student & parent profiles](./09-Student_&_Parent_Profiles.md)** — look up
  student and parent information.

Your **MyDay** home page pulls the day's essentials together; start there and dip
into each feature as you need it.
