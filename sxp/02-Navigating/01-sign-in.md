# Sign in to StaffXP

StaffXP uses your GEMS Microsoft 365 account — the same login you use for email.

1. Sign in with Microsoft

   Open StaffXP and click **Sign in with Microsoft**.

   {/* CAPTURE: the StaffXP sign-in screen with the "Sign in with Microsoft" button */}

2. Enter your details

   Enter your **GEMS M365** email and password, then click **Sign in**.
