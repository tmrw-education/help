# Log a reward or consequence

Felix logs rewards and consequences against a student, works out which of your
school's values the behaviour demonstrates, and creates the postcard that the
student and their parents see.

1. Prompt Felix

   Open **Felix** and say what you want to record — for example *log a reward for
   Sophie for helping other classmates*. You can type it or use **voice input**.

   Giving a reason in the prompt lets Felix suggest the matching value. Without
   one, you choose the value yourself.

2. Confirm the student

   Felix confirms who you mean. If several students match, they are all listed —
   select the right one, or select more than one to log the same thing for each.

3. Check your school's settings

   Felix shows the settings that apply before you commit — whether a
   **notification**, a **postcard** and a **certificate** are sent for this log,
   and whether **teacher reminders** are on.

   > **Note:** These are set per class and per school by your school admin in CE,
   > not by you. Your school may collect logs and send postcards weekly or at a
   > milestone rather than one at a time. See
   > **Configure agent features for behaviour management** in the CE user guide.

4. Accept or change the value and points

   Felix names the value it matched — for example **Care**, worth **2 points**,
   *for offering help to others through acts of kindness or community service* —
   and explains why it chose it. Pick a different value if it is wrong, or switch
   to the **Consequence** tab instead.

   The **running tally** at the bottom shows the student's reward points for the
   year and what this log will take them to.

5. Preview the postcard

   **Preview** shows the postcard as the student sees it in the Learner Experience
   Platform and as the parent sees it in the Parent Experience Platform. **Edit**
   takes you back.

6. Send

   Click **Send**. The postcard is created and logged in CE, and goes out
   according to your school's settings.

## Logging a consequence

A consequence follows the same flow on the **Consequence** tab, with two
differences: the points are deducted from the student's tally, and **a case is
created in CE** so the consequence can be assigned to a member of staff and
tracked until the student's behaviour improves.

## Milestones and certificates

As a student's points accumulate they pass milestones, and a certificate is issued
to the Learner Experience Platform for the student and the Parent Experience
Platform for their parents. GEMS schools use three: **50**, **450** and **1,000**
points. Milestones are configurable per school in CE.

> **Note:** Points are a record of recognition only. They are not a balance and
> cannot be exchanged for anything.
