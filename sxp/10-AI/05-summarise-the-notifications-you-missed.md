# Summarise the notifications you missed

If your notifications have built up, Felix can read them for you and hand back a
short summary of each, so you do not have to work down the whole list.

1. Ask for the summary

   Open **Felix** and prompt *summarise my notifications*, *what notifications did
   I miss?*, or simply *notification summary*.

2. Read the summary

   Felix groups what it finds into **Alerts** and **Messages**. Each item gets a
   one-line summary and how long ago it arrived — for example *two students absent
   on leave without a request, 15 minutes ago*.

3. Open a notification in full

   Click the **footnote** on any item to open that notification in the canvas.

4. Mark it read or unread

   Opening a notification marks it as **read**. Use **Mark as unread** if you want
   to come back to it.

   > **Note:** For the full notifications list, filters and do-not-disturb, see
   > [Check your notifications](../08-Notifications/01-check-notifications.md).
