# Request an extra-curricular activity

Requesting an extra-curricular activity is a long, gated, four-step planning
form — **Activity details → Resource booking → Risk management → Review and
submit** — that goes to a coordinator or leader for approval. Any staff member
with extra-curricular access can request one. A progress indicator sits in the
header, and a fixed action bar with **Save and exit**, **Back**, and **Next**
runs along the bottom. **Submit** stays disabled until every required field
across all four steps is valid; errors show inline as you go.

1. Open the create flow

   From the **Extra curricular** hub, click **Create extra-curricular
   activity** on the **Request an extra-curricular activity** action card. To
   resume a saved draft instead, click **Edit** on its **New/Draft** request row.

   {/* CAPTURE: the Extra-curricular hub with the Create extra-curricular activity action card */}

2. Fill in the activity details (Step 1)

   Complete the three sub-sections in the side rail. In **General information**,
   set the **Extra-curricular name** and **Extra activity type**, then the
   **Event start/end** and **Registration start/end** dates and times — each
   start must be before its end, and start dates can't be in the past. Optionally
   add a **Create a Teams for this activity** space, an **Event image** (≤10 MB,
   .png/.jpg/.jpeg, one file), or **Fixtures** documents (≤10 MB each,
   .pdf/.doc/.docx).

   {/* CAPTURE: Step 1 General information with the event and registration date/time fields */}

3. Set the venue and attendees (Step 1)

   In **Venue details**, choose **On-site location** (add rooms, each with a room,
   configuration, and expected attendees) or **Off-site location** (add venues
   with full contact details); the section is locked with "Please select event
   date first" until the event date is set. In **Attendees**, add at least one
   **Student group**, write the **Student requirements**, set **Max no. of
   students** (greater than 0) and the **Staff to student ratio**, pick **Cost
   to** (Parent, Parents & School, or School) with its matching amount and
   **Payment due date**, and tick at least one **Accessibility considerations**
   option. Turn on **Enable waitlist** to hold students beyond the max.

4. Book resources (Step 2 — optional)

   You can skip this step. To book, add resource rows in **Select resources**
   (each needs a category and a specific resource, plus a quantity greater than 0
   for most categories). If you book anything, **Resource collection** then
   requires **Picked up by**, **Collection date**, and **Return date**.

5. Complete the risk assessment (Step 3)

   Each risk area — **Student list**, **Activity based**, **Transport**, **Staff
   capacity** — must either list risks (each with a mitigation) or be marked as
   no risk; you can't leave one blank. Fill in **Key contacts** (lead, first-aid,
   principal, deputy, plus a local emergency number — all phone-format-checked;
   principal and deputy auto-fill), tick the **External provider** confirmation,
   and in **Requester clarification** tick the requirements confirmation and pick
   a **Pre-event briefing status**.

6. Review and submit (Step 4)

   Check the read-only summary on **Review and submit**, use **Back** to amend
   anything, then click **Submit**. A success message confirms the activity is
   created. Once approved by a coordinator or leader, it opens for enrolment and
   appears on the extra-curricular calendar.

   > **Note:** **Save and exit** keeps a draft you can resume later from its
   > **New/Draft** request row — but it's disabled until the activity has a name.
   > If you navigate away with unsaved changes and no draft, an unsaved-changes
   > prompt warns you before the work is lost.
