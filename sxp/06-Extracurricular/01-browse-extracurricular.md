# Browse extra-curricular activities and requests

The **Extra curricular** hub is where you find the school's clubs and teams —
their requests, bookings, approved activities and assigned staff.

1. Open the hub

   Click **Extra curricular** in the navigation. Two action cards sit at the
   top — **Create extra-curricular activity** and **View calendar** — above a
   set of tabs. Anyone with extra-curricular access can open the hub, but what
   each tab shows depends on your role.

   {/* CAPTURE: Extra curricular hub showing the two action cards above the row of tabs */}

2. Choose a tab

   The first tab is the request queue: regular staff see **Your requests** (your
   own submissions); activity coordinators and school leaders see **All
   requests** (everyone's queue) plus a **My requests** tab that filters the same
   queue to activities you lead. The other tabs are **Booking status**,
   **Supervising activities**, and **Staffing**.

3. Read the request queue

   The request tabs list each request under **Requests** (the name, a link),
   with its **Submitted** date, **Type** and **Status**. **Search** by request
   name and **Filter** by **Status** and **Type** together.

   > **Note:** For approvers, **All requests** opens pre-filtered to **Pending** —
   > it's the triage queue. Clear the **Pending** chip to see every status. Your
   > own **Your requests** / **My requests** always show all statuses.

4. Use the row action that matches the status

   Each request row carries one status-aware action. A **New / Draft** request
   shows **Edit** (pencil) and **Delete** (bin) to its author, and its name
   reopens the editable form. A **Pending** request shows **Review** (only if you
   can approve) and opens the **Review request** screen. An **Approved** request
   shows **Replicate** and its name opens the **Activity details** page. A
   **Declined / Rejected** or **More info** request offers **View review notes**.

5. Check resource bookings

   The **Booking status** tab lists bookings tied to activities, with columns
   **Event**, **Resource**, **Quantity**, **Picked up by**, **Return date** and
   **Status**. Search by event name and filter by booking status.

6. Browse live activities

   The **Supervising activities** tab ("All ECAs") lists only **Approved** and
   **Rejected** activities, with **Name** (a link to its details), **Event
   type**, **Event start**, **Event end**, **Activity lead** and **Status**.
   Search across name, type or lead and filter by **Approved** or **Rejected**.
   Approved rows offer **Replicate**; Rejected rows offer **View review notes**.

7. Look up assigned staff

   The **Staffing** tab lists staff assigned to activities, with **Name**,
   **Role**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Consent**. Search by name and filter by
   role. **Consent** shows **View** to open an external member's clearance
   document, **Internal** for internal staff, or a dash when there's nothing
   to show.

8. Know what each status means

   The coloured **Status** tag tells you the stage: **New / Draft** is saved but
   not yet submitted; **Pending** is submitted and awaiting a decision; **More
   info** means an approver has sent it back with notes for you to update and
   resubmit; **Approved** means it's live and open for enrolment; and **Declined
   / Rejected** was turned down — open **View review notes** to read why.

   {/* CAPTURE: Close-up of the Status column showing the coloured status tags */}

   Every table sets how many rows show per page (10, 25, 50 or 100).
