# School Policy

Here you can view the school's policies; some will be school-wide, while others may be specific to different year levels.

1. Select the School Policy prompt

   Click the **School Information** tile from the home screen. Then select **School Policy** from the suggested prompts. The system will ask you to choose which child you wish to view the policies for. After you choose, the relevant policies will automatically display in a table.
