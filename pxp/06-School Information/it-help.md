# IT Help

If your child is having any issues with their technology, this feature helps them get the support they need from the school.

1. Select IT Help from the prompts

   Click the **School Information** tile. Select **IT Help** from the suggested prompts. Select which child needs support. You can draft an email to the IT Help desk through the app. Select the **Type of issue** from the dropdown menu.
