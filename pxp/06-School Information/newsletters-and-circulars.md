# Newsletters and Circulars

The school sends newsletters and circulars that outline important information and developments. Use this feature to keep track of these communications.

1. Select Newsletters and Circulars

   Click **School Information** from the home screen tiles. Select **Newsletters and Circulars** from the suggested prompts. Some newsletters will be school-wide; some will be specific to an age group. Choose your child from the list to view the relevant communications. Click **View newsletters and circulars**.
