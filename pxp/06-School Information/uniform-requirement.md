# Uniform Requirement

Different age groups have different uniform requirements; find out what's appropriate for your child here.

1. Select the uniform requirement from the prompts

   Click the **School Information** tile. Select the **Uniform Requirement** from the suggested prompts. Choose your child from the list. The uniform policy will display.
