# School Calendar

This feature lets you view the start and end dates of terms, exam weeks, and public holidays.

1. Select School Calendar from the prompts

   Click **School Information** on the home screen tiles. Select **School calendar** from the suggested prompts. Choose your child's name from the list to view their school calendar.
