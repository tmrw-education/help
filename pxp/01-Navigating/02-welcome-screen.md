# Welcome Screen

1. Sign in to the ParentExperience (PXP) app

   After signing in, the app will automatically take you to the Welcome screen.

   ![ParentExperience app welcome screen](./images/welcome-screen-1.png)
   
3. Watch the video

   Click **Watch video** to learn about some of the app functionalities.

   > **Note:** Select **Don't show video again** to go straight to the app home screen in the future and bypass this welcome screen.
