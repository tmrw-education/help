# Early Payment Discount

In some cases, you may be eligible for a discount if you settle payment early.

> **Note:** You must clear all outstanding fees to be eligible for an early payment discount.

1. Select the Fees tile

   Tap the **Fees** tile on the home screen. Select **Early payment discount**. Read the instructions and eligibility guidelines for the early payment discount.
