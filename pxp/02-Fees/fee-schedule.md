# Fee Schedule

The fee schedule outlines the dates for paying school fees per term.

1. Select Fees Schedule from the list of prompts

   Click the **Fees** tile from the home screen. Then select the **Fees Schedule (current academic year)** from the list of Nova prompts. The fee payment schedule will load automatically.
