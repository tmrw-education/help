# Registered Activities

Use this space to check which activities your children are already registered to participate in.

1. Select Registered Activities from the suggested prompts

   Click **Events and Activities** from the home screen tiles. Select **Registered Activities** from the suggested prompts. Choose the child whose events you want to see. The activities page will automatically load.
