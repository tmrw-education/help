# View Activities

Use this page to search for upcoming and current activities your child is enrolled in or interested in enrolling in.

1. Select View Activities from the suggested prompts

   Click **Events and Activities** from the home screen tiles. Select **View Activities** from the suggested prompts. Choose the child whose events you want to see. The activities page will automatically load.
