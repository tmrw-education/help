# Becoming an Activity Volunteer

Use this page to volunteer your time and support for a particular activity or event.

1. Select Becoming activity volunteer from the suggested prompts

   Click **Events and Activities** from the home screen tiles. Select **Becoming activity volunteer** from the suggested prompts. Choose the child whose events you want to see. The activities page will automatically load. Select the event you would like to volunteer for. Follow the prompts to sign up.
