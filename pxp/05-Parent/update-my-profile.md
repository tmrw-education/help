# Update My Profile

This feature lets you update any information shared with the school, usually contact details and emergency information.

1. Select View My Profile

   Click the **Parent** tile from the home screen. Tap the **View My Profile** prompt. Click the bar reading **View parent profile**. Your profile will display automatically.

2. Update the details and share with the school

   You can edit the fields in the profile. When you update them, the arrow at the top right activates. Click the arrow to save and share the updates.
