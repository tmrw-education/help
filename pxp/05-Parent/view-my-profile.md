# View My Profile

Your profile shows the information you shared with the school, including contact details like an email address and emergency number.

1. Select View My Profile

   Click the **Parent** tile from the home screen. Tap the **View My Profile** prompt. Click the bar reading **View parent profile**. Your profile will display automatically. Tap the pen icon at the top right to edit the profile details.
