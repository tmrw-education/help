# View Medical Information

This feature displays your child's medical history shared with the school.

1. Click View Medical Information

   Click the **Student** tile on the home screen. Then select **View Medical Information** from the prompts. If you have more than one child enrolled in the school, their names will appear in a list. Tap their name to view their profile. The medical page will open automatically.
