# Update Medical Information

This feature allows you to update or add new medical information to the records already shared with the school.

1. Select Update medical information from the prompts

   After clicking the **Student** tile on the home screen, select the **Update medical information** prompt. The names of all children enrolled in the school will appear. Select the child whose information you need to update.

2. Update the medical records

   The medical records will open automatically. You can edit or remove the existing information. If new information is to be added, click **+** in the top right corner and complete the **New Medical Details** form. Share by pressing the arrow.

   > **Note:** The arrow activates when the fields in the form are complete.
