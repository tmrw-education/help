# Teacher List

Here is a list of your child's teachers. The app will help you draft an email if you wish to reach out to one.

1. Select Teacher List from the suggested prompts

   Click the **Timetable** tile from the home screen. Select **Teacher List** from the suggested prompts. Select the child whose teachers you want to see. The list of teachers will automatically appear.
