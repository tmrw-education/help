# PE Today

Use this feature to see if your child has PE (physical education) today and needs to pack their sportswear for school.

1. Select the PE Today prompt

   Click the **Timetable** tile on the home screen. Select **PE Today** from the suggested prompts. Choose the child whose schedule you want to check. Their timetable will open automatically. Scroll all the way through the day to see all the classes.
