# View Exam Timetable

You can check the exam timetable for all your children enrolled in the school here.

1. Select View Exam Timetable from the suggested prompts

   Click the **Timetable** tile on the home screen. Select **View Exam Timetable** from the suggested prompts. Choose the child whose timetable you wish to see. The schedule will automatically appear.

   > **Note:** The exam timetable will appear only when the school has set it.
