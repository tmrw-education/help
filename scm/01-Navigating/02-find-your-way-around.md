# Find your way around

The **navigation bar** sits across the top of every screen. Here's what's on it, left to right.

1. The home link

   **Procurement XP** (far left) is your home — click it any time to return to the overview page.

   ![The Procurement XP top navigation bar showing section links across the top and the Notifications and User menu icons at the right](./images/find-your-way-around-1.png)

2. The section links

   The links across the top take you to each area of the platform:

   - **Vendors** — your approved and active vendor directory.
   - **Prospective Vendors** — vendors working through the registration and review process.
   - **Contracts** — create, manage, and track contracts.
   - **Purchase Requisitions** — raise and manage purchase requisitions.
   - **Projects** — sourcing and procurement projects, including bidding and auctions.

3. The far-right icons

   - **Notifications** (bell) — alerts for actions that need your attention.
   - **User menu** (person) — access your profile, adjust settings, or sign out.
