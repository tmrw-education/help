# Sign out

End your session — worth doing on any shared or public device.

1. Click the **User** icon (top right).

2. Click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the menu. You're returned to the sign-in page.

   > **Note:** Always sign out on shared devices to keep your procurement data secure.
