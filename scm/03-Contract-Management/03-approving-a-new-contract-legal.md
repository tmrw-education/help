

# Approving a new contract - Legal

Some new contracts require alignment with the school's legal framework and the safeguarding of its assets. As the assigned legal team member, you will be responsible for this task.

1. Review the contract information already in the form

   The contract **Initiator** or **Contract manager** will pre-fill the fields.
   
3. Update and upload the contract

   Add necessary modifications and upload this latest version in the **Attachment** tab, under the **Contract Version Upload**.

4. Approve the contract

   This forwards the contract to the assigned Business team member.

   > **Note:** If more edits are needed, use the **Send back...** button to reassign the contract to the **Initiator** or **Contract manager**.
