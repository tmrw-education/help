Steps component demo

A numbered procedure with the connecting rail. Title and body stay visible; only StepDetail collapses.

  1. Open the Register

    Go to StaffXP → Register and choose today's class from the dropdown.

  2. Mark each learner

    Tap a learner to cycle present, late, or absent. Changes save automatically as you go.

    Press P, L, or A with a learner selected to mark them without the mouse. Use the arrow keys to move between learners.

  3. Confirm the summary

    A summary appears at the top once every learner is accounted for. Check the totals, then you're done.
