# Fee Payer Statements

This section explains how to generate a fee payer account statement for a selected period. It covers choosing the date range, deciding whether to include payment options, filtering for a specific fee payer account, and generating and distributing the completed statement.

---


#### Statements
Fee payer account statements provide a summarised view of a fee payer's financial activity over a selected period, including invoices raised, payments received, and any outstanding balances. Staff generate the statement by selecting the date range, deciding whether to include payment option details, and filtering for the specific fee payer account. The system compiles the statement and presents it for review before it is distributed to the fee payer. These statements are a useful tool for keeping fee payers informed of their account status and for resolving any billing queries or discrepancies.


---

## Generate Fee Statements

1. From the **FNO dashboard**, open **Modules** ▸ **Academic Management**.
2. Expand **Inquiries and reports**, then expand **Fee payer statement report**.
3. Click **Fee payer account statement**.
4. Enter the **start and end dates** for the statement period you want to generate.
5. Decide whether to show **payment options** on the statement by selecting the appropriate option.
6. Open the **Records to include** section and click **Filter** to choose the specific account for which you want to generate the statement.
7. Click **OK** to generate the statement for the selected account.
8. Review the completed statement and distribute it to the fee payer as required.
