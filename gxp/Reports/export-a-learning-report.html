Export a learning report

Quick answer

  • In Learning reports, set People and the filters.
  • Click Export detail for one row per person and course, or Export summary for the totals.
  • Large reports are prepared in the background and appear under Exports to download.
  • Learning managers and report viewers export the learning of the organisations they're given.

You can download a learning report as a CSV file. You need the Line manager, Learning manager, Report viewer, HR manager or Organisation administrator role. Learning managers and report viewers export the learning of the organisations they're given, and of their child units only if their access includes them.

  1. Set up the report

    In Manage ▸ Learning reports, choose People and set the filters. The export contains exactly what you've filtered.

  2. Choose the export

    Click Export detail for one row per person and course, including completion source, expiry, feedback and certificate number. Click Export summary for the totals by course, school and staff category.

  3. Download it

    For up to 2,000 people the file downloads straight away. For more, a message says Your export is being prepared and the file appears under Exports at the bottom of the page as Detail export or Summary export. Its Status goes from Queued to Preparing… to Ready. Then click the Download icon within 24 hours.

    Note: Only you can download your export, and each file is deleted when it expires, 24 hours after it's ready. Download it before the date under Available until.

  4. Tidy up

    Click the delete icon to Cancel export while it's being prepared, or Delete file once it's ready. You can have up to three exports being prepared at once.
