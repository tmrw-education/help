# How line manager access works

If people report to you, GrowthXP gives you the **Line manager** role automatically, so you can follow
their learning. This page explains who's in your team and what you can see.

1. Know who's in your team

   Your team is everyone who reports to you directly, plus everyone who reports to them, all the way down. The reporting
   relationships come from your HR system, or an organisation administrator sets them on the **People** page. When
   someone joins or leaves your team, your access changes straight away.

2. Know when you get the role

   You get the **Line manager** role as soon as one person reports to you, and you lose it when nobody does. Nobody can
   give or remove it by hand on **Access and permissions**. If someone is missing from your team, ask your organisation
   administrator to check that person's line manager on **People**.

3. Find your team's learning

   Click **Manage** in the header. In the side navigation under **Organisation** you'll see **My team**, **Deadlines
   calendar** and **Learning reports**. If line manager access is your only management access, **Manage** opens on
   **My team**.

   ![The Manage side navigation for a line manager, showing My team, Deadlines calendar and Learning reports under Organisation](./images/gxp-how-line-manager-access-works-1.png)

4. Know what you can and can't do

   You can see each person's progress, mandatory compliance, due dates, validity, feedback status and certificates. You
   can open their certificates and export your team's learning to a CSV file. You can't assign, remove or change
   anyone's learning: ask a learning manager, HR manager or organisation administrator to do that. You never see the
   comments people write in course feedback, only whether they've given it.
