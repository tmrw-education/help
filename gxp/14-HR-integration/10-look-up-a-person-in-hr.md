# Look up a person in HR

Find one person in HR to see how the import rules apply to them, or to bring a new starter in now instead of waiting for
the next full sync.

You need the **Organisation administrator** role.

1. Search HR

   Go to **Manage** → **Settings** → **Synchronisation** → **People** and find **Individual HR lookup**. Enter a name or
   personnel number in **Name or personnel number** (at least 2 characters) and click **Search HR**. For an exact
   personnel number, click **Check number** instead. This also finds people who don't appear in the search.

2. Check the person

   In the results, click the check icon on the person's row. The check shows their **Account**, **Current organisation**,
   **HR status**, where their legal entities come from, and the **Proposed result**: for example **Create account**,
   **No change**, **Move to new legal entities**, **Re-enable account** or **Not imported** with the reason.

   ![An individual check for Alex Morgan showing the account, HR status and proposed result](./images/gxp-look-up-a-person-in-hr-1.png)

3. Import a missing person

   If they don't have an account yet and can be imported, click **Import user**. Their account is created now, without
   waiting for the next sync. **Import user** only creates missing accounts. Changes to existing people are made by the
   next full user sync.

   You can't import someone while a user sync is running, paused or waiting for review. Searching and checking still work.
