# Export a learning report

You can download a learning report as a CSV file. You need the **Line manager**, **HR manager** or **Organisation
administrator** role. Learning managers and report viewers can read reports but can't export them, so they don't see
the export buttons.

1. Set up the report

   In **Manage** ▸ **Learning reports**, choose **People** and set the filters. The export contains exactly what you've
   filtered.

2. Choose the export

   Click **Export detail** for one row per person and course, including completion source, expiry, feedback and
   certificate number. Click **Export summary** for the totals by course, school and staff category.

   ![The Export detail and Export summary buttons above the report totals](./images/gxp-export-a-learning-report-1.png)

3. Download it

   For up to 2,000 people the file downloads straight away. For more, a message says **Your export is being prepared**
   and the file appears under **Exports** at the bottom of the page as **Detail export** or **Summary export**. Its
   **Status** goes from **Queued** to **Preparing…** to **Ready**. Then click the **Download** icon within 24 hours.

   > **Note:** Only you can download your export, and each file is deleted when it expires, 24 hours after it's
   > ready. Download it before the date under **Available until**.

4. Tidy up

   Click the delete icon to **Cancel export** while it's being prepared, or **Delete file** once it's ready. You can
   have up to three exports being prepared at once.
