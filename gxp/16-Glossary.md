# Glossary

Terms used across GrowthXP and this guide.

## A

### Access and permissions

The **Manage** page where organisation administrators see who has which role (**Who has access**), give and remove roles (**Give access**), and check what each role can do (**Roles explained**).

### Assigned by

Where an assignment came from: a learning rule, an administrator or manager, a bulk assignment, HR, or the learner enrolling themselves. When several sources require the same learning, it shows how many.

### Assignment

A course or pathway given to a person, with a classification and, usually, a due date. A person has one current assignment per course, whatever its sources.

### Audience

The schools or organisations whose people can see a course. A course can be open to everyone, or only to chosen units and the units below them.

### Audit log

The record of administrative actions and changes: who did what, to what, and when. Read only; the latest 500 entries are shown.

## B

### Backfill

Applying a new learning rule to people who already matched it before the rule was activated, after previewing who it would assign.

### Bulk assignment

Assigning a course to everyone in one or more organisations at once. It runs in the background, and its results are kept in **Bulk assignment history**.

## C

### Certificate

Proof of completion, issued automatically when a learner completes a course that has a certificate, or after they give feedback if feedback is required. Each certificate has its own shareable page.

### Certificate template

The design a certificate is printed from. Each edit creates a new version, and issued certificates keep the version they were issued with.

### Channel

A collection of courses on a theme, such as Compliance, that learners browse in **Courses**.

### Classification

Whether an assignment is **Mandatory** or **Recommended**. Classification belongs to the assignment or the learning rule, not the course, so the same course can be mandatory for some people and recommended for others. Older learning saved before classification existed shows **Not set**.

### Completion source

Whether a completion was recorded in the platform or in HR. Exports show it as **LMS** or **HR record**.

### Course manager

A role for people who create, edit and publish courses, channels, learning pathways and certificate templates, choose who can see each course, and revoke awarded certificates. Covers the whole organisation.

### Cycle

One round of a piece of learning that must be renewed. When a completion is about to expire, the next cycle is assigned, with its own due date. Earlier cycles stay in the person's training history.

## D

### Deadlines calendar

The **Manage** calendar of assignment, feedback, expiry and renewal dates for the people you can see. Learners have their own **Calendar**.

### Disabled

An account that can't sign in. Disabled people get no new learning or emails, but their learning records, completions and certificates are kept. Disabled by an administrator or by the HR sync, and never deleted.

### Due date

When an assignment should be completed. Mandatory learning past its due date is **Overdue**. Recommended learning is never overdue.

## E

### Escalation

See *Manager escalation*.

### Expired

Completed learning whose validity has ended. Mandatory learning that has expired needs to be completed again.

### Expiring soon

Completed learning that will expire within the organisation's warning period, usually 30 days.

### Expiry

The date a completion stops being valid, worked out from the completion date and the validity period. Not to be confused with a course's own end date, after which the course is no longer available.

## F

### F&O

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations, the first HR system the HR integration connects to.

### Feedback deadline

How many days after completion course feedback is due. Late feedback is still accepted and marked as late.

### From HR

A tag on something that came from your HR system, such as a course draft, a staff category or an organisation membership. Things from HR are changed in HR, not in the platform.

## G

### Grace days

Days added to a learning rule's starting date to give the due date. For example, a new joiner's employment start date plus 30 days.

## H

### HR course title

The name your HR learning record uses for a course in a legal entity. Mapped to a platform course so learner status and completions can be matched.

### HR integration

The connection between the platform and your HR system that syncs people, organisations, courses, enrolments and learning records.

### HR manager

A role for people who assign learning to anyone, change due dates, manage learning rules, and run and export learning reports. Covers the whole organisation.

### HR-linked

A person whose account is matched to a worker in HR. Only HR-linked people are disabled by the HR sync when they leave HR.

## I

### Import rules

The rules the user sync follows: active workers only, occupied current positions, temporary positions left out, and placement by current employment. Set with TMRW support.

### Individual HR lookup

A tool in **Synchronisation** for finding one person in HR, seeing what the next sync will do to them, and importing a missing person straight away.

## L

### Learner

Everyone who signs in. Learners take courses, give feedback, and see their own learning, certificates and calendar.

### Learning assignment

See *Assignment*. **Learning assignments** is also the **Manage** page that lists who has which learning.

### Learning manager

A role for people who add people to organisations, assign and remove learning, and follow and export progress and reports for the units they're given.

### Learning pathway

An ordered set of courses, sometimes with prerequisites, that learners follow towards a goal.

### Learning rule

A rule that assigns courses automatically, as mandatory or recommended, to everyone who matches conditions such as organisation, staff category or length of service. Rules re-check people when their details change.

### Legal entity

A company or school in your HR system. Imported from HR and linked to a unit in the organisation structure, so people are placed in the right unit.

### Line manager

Someone with people reporting to them. Line managers see their team's learning automatically, for their direct reports and everyone below them. This role is never given by hand.

### Line population

The people a line manager can see: their direct reports and everyone below them in the manager hierarchy.

## M

### Mandatory

Learning a person must complete by its due date. Mandatory learning can become overdue, is reminded and escalated, and counts towards compliance. When sources disagree, mandatory wins.

### Manager escalation

An optional email to a person's line manager when their mandatory learning is still overdue after a set number of delivered overdue reminders. Off by default.

### Membership

A person's place in an organisation unit. Memberships come from HR (**From HR**) or are added by an administrator (**Added by admin**).

### Moved

A person the HR sync placed in a different legal entity. Their old HR memberships end and new ones begin.

## N

### Not set

The classification shown for older learning saved before mandatory and recommended existed. It's never guessed.

### Not signed in yet

A person imported from HR who hasn't signed in for the first time. They can be given learning, memberships and roles before they sign in.

## O

### Organisation

The whole customer, or one unit in its structure, such as a trust, a group of schools or a school. "Whole organisation" means everyone.

### Organisation administrator

The role that runs the whole platform: courses, learning, people, access, notifications, settings and the HR integration.

### Organisation structure

The tree of units, such as groups and schools, used to scope access, reports and learning rules.

### Overdue

Mandatory learning past its due date and not completed. Recommended learning is never overdue.

## P

### Pathway

See *Learning pathway*.

### People needing review

People whose staff category isn't defined, or who have conflicting categories from HR. Learning rules don't treat them as any category until it's resolved.

## R

### Recommended

Learning a person is encouraged to complete. It's never overdue and isn't escalated, and it only has a due date if one is set.

### Recorded in HR

A completion imported from your HR system for learning completed outside the platform. It counts as completed, but there's no certificate or evidence in the platform.

### Renewal

Assigning learning again before its completion expires, as the next cycle. The renewal is assigned a set number of days before expiry (the renewal lead time) and is due on the expiry date.

### Report viewer

A role for people who see and export learning progress and reports, and see the deadlines calendar, for the units they're given, without changing anything.

### Review limit

The share of HR-linked people (5% by default) or number of people (200 by default) above which a user sync stops for review before disabling anyone.

### Roles explained

The tab on **Access and permissions** that shows what each role can do, built from the permissions the platform enforces.

## S

### Scope

Where a role applies: the whole organisation, or one unit with or without its child units. Also the organisation you choose to look at in reports and calendars.

### SCORM

The standard format for e-learning packages. Courses are uploaded as SCORM packages and play in the platform.

### Self-enrolment

A learner enrolling themselves in a published course that allows it. Self-enrolled learning is recommended.

### Staff category

A group such as Teacher, Teaching assistant, Leadership or Support staff, used by learning rules, filters and reports. Each has a code that matches your HR system.

### Sync history

The list of HR syncs, scheduled and manual, with what each changed. The people in each user sync are kept for 7 days or while it's one of the latest 7 runs.

## U

### Unit

One place in the organisation structure, such as a school. Roles, reports and learning rules can be limited to a unit, with or without the units below it.

### User sync

The HR sync that creates and updates people from HR, places them in the right units, and disables people no longer in HR.

## V

### Validity

How long a completion stays valid, for example 12 months. Set on the course or the learning rule, with an organisation default for mandatory learning. Some learning never expires.

## W

### Whole organisation

Everyone in your organisation. Organisation administrator, Course manager and HR manager always cover the whole organisation; Learning manager and Report viewer can too.
