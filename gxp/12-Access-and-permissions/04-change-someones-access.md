# Change someone's role or where it applies

To change a person's role, or the unit a role covers, give the new access first and then remove the old one. Doing it in
this order means they never lose access in between.

You need the **Organisation administrator** role.

1. Give the new access

   Go to **Manage** → **Access and permissions**, click **Give access**, and give the person the new role, or the same
   role for the new unit. See [Give someone access](./03-give-someone-access.md).

2. Find the old access

   In **Who has access**, search for the person by name. Both rows are listed, each with its **Where**.

3. Remove the old row

   Click the remove icon on the old row, then **Remove access**. Their account, their other access and their learning
   records stay. See [Remove someone's access](./05-remove-someones-access.md).
