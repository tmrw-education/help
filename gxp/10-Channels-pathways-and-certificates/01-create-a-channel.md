# Create a channel

Channels group courses by topic, such as "Compliance", so learners can browse them on **Courses**. You need the
**Course manager** or **Organisation administrator** role.

1. Open Channels

   Click **Manage** in the header, then **Channels** under **Content**. If there are none yet, the page says "No
   channels yet. Create one to organise your content."

2. Create the channel

   Click **Create channel** at the top of the page. In **Create topic channel**, enter the **Channel name** and a
   **Description**.

   ![The Create topic channel window with Channel name, Description and the Mandatory switch](./images/gxp-create-a-channel-1.png)

3. Decide on the Mandatory label

   The **Mandatory** switch only labels the channel as **Mandatory** or **Optional** on this page. It doesn't assign
   its courses or make them mandatory for anyone: use an assignment or a learning rule for that.

4. Create it

   Click **Create**. The channel appears in the list. Next,
   [add courses to it](./02-add-courses-to-a-channel.md). Channel names can't be changed later, so check the name
   before you create it.
