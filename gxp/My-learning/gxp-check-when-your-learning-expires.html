Check when your learning expires

Quick answer

  • Completed cards in My courses show Valid until, Expires, Expired or No expiry.
  • Validity counts whole months from the day you completed the course, such as 12 months.
  • Expiry dates also appear in Calendar.

Some learning, such as first aid or safeguarding, is only valid for a set time after you complete it.

  1. Look at the completed card

    In My courses, click the Completed tab. Each card shows its validity next to the completion date:

    • Valid until and a date: your completion is current.
    • Expires and a date: it expires soon.
    • Expired and a date: it has expired.
    • No expiry: it never needs renewing.

  2. See expiry dates in your calendar

    Calendar shows each Expiry, and for mandatory learning the date its Renewal due starts.

  3. Know how it's worked out

    The validity runs for whole calendar months from the moment you completed the course, for example 12 months. A completion on 29 February expires on 28 February. The length is set by the course or by your organisation, and it's fixed when you complete: later changes to the setting don't move your expiry date.

Expired learning still shows as Completed; only its validity changes. Mandatory learning is usually assigned to you again before it expires. See Renew your learning. Dates are the same for everyone, whatever your time zone.
