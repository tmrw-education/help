# Sign in to GXP

GXP uses your organisation's Microsoft account, the same one you use for email. There's no separate GXP password.

1. Click Sign in

   Open GXP and click **Sign in**. The hint underneath reads **Use your organisation login**.

   ![The GXP sign-in page with the Sign in button](./images/gxp-sign-in-1.png)

2. Choose your account

   Microsoft's account picker opens. Choose your organisation account, or enter it, and complete Microsoft's sign-in,
   including any verification your organisation uses.

3. Wait for your learning to load

   GXP shows **Welcome to tmrw** while it loads your learning, then opens on **My courses**.

4. If GXP shows a message instead

   - **Your organisation isn’t licensed**: GXP isn't available for the organisation of the account you used. Click
     **Sign in with another account** if you have a different work account, or ask your administrator.
   - **Your account has been disabled**: you can't use GXP at the moment, for example because you no longer appear in
     your organisation's HR system. Your learning records are kept. Contact your administrator.
   - **We couldn’t sign you in** or **We couldn’t verify your access**: click **Try again**. If it keeps happening,
     contact your IT support team.
