# What is GrowthXP?

**GrowthXP** is your organisation's learning platform. It holds the training and professional
development courses you're asked to complete, and the ones you can choose for yourself. It keeps track of your progress,
due dates, certificates and when your learning needs renewing.

1. Your learning in one place

   **My courses** is your home page. It lists every course you've been given, and every course you've started, with
   your progress and due dates. Courses are online modules that play inside GrowthXP and save your place as you go.

   ![The My courses page with the learning summary, status tabs and course cards](./images/gxp-what-is-gxp-1.png)

2. Courses you can choose

   **Courses** is the catalogue of everything offered to you, grouped into channels such as **Compliance**. Some courses
   let you enrol yourself.

3. Mandatory and recommended learning

   Learning you're given is either **Mandatory** (you must complete it by its due date, and you'll be reminded) or
   **Recommended** (worth doing, but never overdue). Some mandatory learning expires after a set time, such as 12 months,
   and is given to you again before it runs out.

4. Pathways, certificates and deadlines

   **Learning pathways** set courses in an order that leads to a certification. **Certificates** holds the certificates
   you earn, ready to download or print. **Calendar** shows every learning due date, feedback date, expiry and renewal in
   a month view.

5. Connected to HR

   GrowthXP can connect to your organisation's HR system, such as Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations. When it does,
   your organisation, line manager and some of your learning come from HR, and your progress can be sent back to your HR
   record.

6. Managers and administrators

   If you manage people or run learning for your organisation, you'll also see **Manage** in the header. What you can do
   there depends on your role.
