# Emails you receive about your learning

GXP sends emails about your learning from your organisation's sender name. Your organisation chooses which ones are
sent and can change their wording, so yours may differ from the defaults below.

1. When learning is assigned to you

   **New learning assigned: (course)** arrives when a course is assigned to you personally, with an **Open course**
   button. You don't get it for courses you enrol in yourself, or for learning given to a whole group.

2. Before and after the due date

   **Learning reminder: (course)** arrives before learning is due, by default 14 days and 3 days before. If mandatory
   learning becomes overdue, you get an overdue notice, by default every 7 days, up to 4 times. Recommended learning is
   never overdue, so it never gets overdue notices. If your organisation has turned on manager escalation, your line
   manager is also told when mandatory learning is still overdue after a set number of notices.

3. When you finish

   **Course completed: (course)** confirms a completion. If the course awards a certificate, **Your certificate:
   (course)** follows, with a **View certificate** button that opens the certificate page.

4. When learning needs renewing

   **Renewal due: (course)** arrives when learning that's about to expire is assigned to you again, with the new due
   date.

5. When feedback is due

   If your organisation sets a deadline for course feedback, **Feedback due: (course)** reminds you before the deadline
   (by default 3 days and 1 day before) and once afterwards, with a **Give feedback** button.

Reminder and overdue emails are sent once a day, only when your organisation has scheduled reminders switched on. If you
aren't getting emails you expect, check your junk folder, then ask your organisation administrator. Administrators set
these emails up in [Choose which learner emails are sent](../13-Organisation-settings/02-choose-learner-emails.md).
