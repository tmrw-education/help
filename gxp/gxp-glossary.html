Glossary

Terms used across GrowthXP and this guide.

A · B · C · D · E · F · G · H · I · L · M · N · O · P · R · S · U · V · W

A​

TermDefinition
Access and permissionsThe Manage page where organisation administrators see who has which role (Who has access), give and remove roles (Give access), and check what each role can do (Roles explained).
Assigned byWhere an assignment came from: a learning rule, an administrator or manager, a bulk assignment, HR, or the learner enrolling themselves. When several sources require the same learning, it shows how many.
AssignmentA course or pathway given to a person, with a classification and, usually, a due date. A person has one current assignment per course, whatever its sources.
AudienceThe schools or organisations whose people can see a course. A course can be open to everyone, or only to chosen units and the units below them.
Audit logThe record of administrative actions and changes: who did what, to what, and when. Read only; the latest 500 entries are shown.

B​

TermDefinition
BackfillApplying a new learning rule to people who already matched it before the rule was activated, after previewing who it would assign.
Bulk assignmentAssigning a course to everyone in one or more organisations at once. It runs in the background, and its results are kept in Bulk assignment history.

C​

TermDefinition
CertificateProof of completion, issued automatically when a learner completes a course that has a certificate, or after they give feedback if feedback is required. Each certificate has its own shareable page.
Certificate templateThe design a certificate is printed from. Each edit creates a new version, and issued certificates keep the version they were issued with.
ChannelA collection of courses on a theme, such as Compliance, that learners browse in Courses.
ClassificationWhether an assignment is Mandatory or Recommended. Classification belongs to the assignment or the learning rule, not the course, so the same course can be mandatory for some people and recommended for others. Older learning saved before classification existed shows Not set.
Completion sourceWhether a completion was recorded in the platform or in HR. Exports show it as LMS or HR record.
Course managerA role for people who create, edit and publish courses, channels, learning pathways and certificate templates, choose who can see each course, and revoke awarded certificates. Covers the whole organisation.
CycleOne round of a piece of learning that must be renewed. When a completion is about to expire, the next cycle is assigned, with its own due date. Earlier cycles stay in the person's training history.

D​

TermDefinition
Deadlines calendarThe Manage calendar of assignment, feedback, expiry and renewal dates for the people you can see. Learners have their own Calendar.
DisabledAn account that can't sign in. Disabled people get no new learning or emails, but their learning records, completions and certificates are kept. Disabled by an administrator or by the HR sync, and never deleted.
Due dateWhen an assignment should be completed. Mandatory learning past its due date is Overdue. Recommended learning is never overdue.

E​

TermDefinition
EscalationSee Manager escalation.
ExpiredCompleted learning whose validity has ended. Mandatory learning that has expired needs to be completed again.
Expiring soonCompleted learning that will expire within the organisation's warning period, usually 30 days.
ExpiryThe date a completion stops being valid, worked out from the completion date and the validity period. Not to be confused with a course's own end date, after which the course is no longer available.

F​

TermDefinition
F&OMicrosoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations, the first HR system the HR integration connects to.
Feedback deadlineHow many days after completion course feedback is due. Late feedback is still accepted and marked as late.
From HRA tag on something that came from your HR system, such as a course draft, a staff category or an organisation membership. Things from HR are changed in HR, not in the platform.

G​

TermDefinition
Grace daysDays added to a learning rule's starting date to give the due date. For example, a new joiner's employment start date plus 30 days.

H​

TermDefinition
HR course titleThe name your HR learning record uses for a course in a legal entity. Mapped to a platform course so learner status and completions can be matched.
HR integrationThe connection between the platform and your HR system that syncs people, organisations, courses, enrolments and learning records.
HR managerA role for people who assign learning to anyone, change due dates, manage learning rules, and run and export learning reports. Covers the whole organisation.
HR-linkedA person whose account is matched to a worker in HR. Only HR-linked people are disabled by the HR sync when they leave HR.

I​

TermDefinition
Import rulesThe rules the user sync follows: active workers only, occupied current positions, temporary positions left out, and placement by current employment. Set with TMRW support.
Individual HR lookupA tool in Synchronisation for finding one person in HR, seeing what the next sync will do to them, and importing a missing person straight away.

L​

TermDefinition
LearnerEveryone who signs in. Learners take courses, give feedback, and see their own learning, certificates and calendar.
Learning assignmentSee Assignment. Learning assignments is also the Manage page that lists who has which learning.
Learning managerA role for people who add people to organisations, assign and remove learning, and follow and export progress and reports for the units they're given.
Learning pathwayAn ordered set of courses, sometimes with prerequisites, that learners follow towards a goal.
Learning ruleA rule that assigns courses automatically, as mandatory or recommended, to everyone who matches conditions such as organisation, staff category or length of service. Rules re-check people when their details change.
Legal entityA company or school in your HR system. Imported from HR and linked to a unit in the organisation structure, so people are placed in the right unit.
Line managerSomeone with people reporting to them. Line managers see their team's learning automatically, for their direct reports and everyone below them. This role is never given by hand.
Line populationThe people a line manager can see: their direct reports and everyone below them in the manager hierarchy.

M​

TermDefinition
MandatoryLearning a person must complete by its due date. Mandatory learning can become overdue, is reminded and escalated, and counts towards compliance. When sources disagree, mandatory wins.
Manager escalationAn optional email to a person's line manager when their mandatory learning is still overdue after a set number of delivered overdue reminders. Off by default.
MembershipA person's place in an organisation unit. Memberships come from HR (From HR) or are added by an administrator (Added by admin).
MovedA person the HR sync placed in a different legal entity. Their old HR memberships end and new ones begin.

N​

TermDefinition
Not setThe classification shown for older learning saved before mandatory and recommended existed. It's never guessed.
Not signed in yetA person imported from HR who hasn't signed in for the first time. They can be given learning, memberships and roles before they sign in.

O​

TermDefinition
OrganisationThe whole customer, or one unit in its structure, such as a trust, a group of schools or a school. "Whole organisation" means everyone.
Organisation administratorThe role that runs the whole platform: courses, learning, people, access, notifications, settings and the HR integration.
Organisation structureThe tree of units, such as groups and schools, used to scope access, reports and learning rules.
OverdueMandatory learning past its due date and not completed. Recommended learning is never overdue.

P​

TermDefinition
PathwaySee Learning pathway.
People needing reviewPeople whose staff category isn't defined, or who have conflicting categories from HR. Learning rules don't treat them as any category until it's resolved.

R​

TermDefinition
RecommendedLearning a person is encouraged to complete. It's never overdue and isn't escalated, and it only has a due date if one is set.
Recorded in HRA completion imported from your HR system for learning completed outside the platform. It counts as completed, but there's no certificate or evidence in the platform.
RenewalAssigning learning again before its completion expires, as the next cycle. The renewal is assigned a set number of days before expiry (the renewal lead time) and is due on the expiry date.
Report viewerA role for people who see and export learning progress and reports, and see the deadlines calendar, for the units they're given, without changing anything.
Review limitThe share of HR-linked people (5% by default) or number of people (200 by default) above which a user sync stops for review before disabling anyone.
Roles explainedThe tab on Access and permissions that shows what each role can do, built from the permissions the platform enforces.

S​

TermDefinition
ScopeWhere a role applies: the whole organisation, or one unit with or without its child units. Also the organisation you choose to look at in reports and calendars.
SCORMThe standard format for e-learning packages. Courses are uploaded as SCORM packages and play in the platform.
Self-enrolmentA learner enrolling themselves in a published course that allows it. Self-enrolled learning is recommended.
Staff categoryA group such as Teacher, Teaching assistant, Leadership or Support staff, used by learning rules, filters and reports. Each has a code that matches your HR system.
Sync historyThe list of HR syncs, scheduled and manual, with what each changed. The people in each user sync are kept for 7 days or while it's one of the latest 7 runs.

U​

TermDefinition
UnitOne place in the organisation structure, such as a school. Roles, reports and learning rules can be limited to a unit, with or without the units below it.
User syncThe HR sync that creates and updates people from HR, places them in the right units, and disables people no longer in HR.

V​

TermDefinition
ValidityHow long a completion stays valid, for example 12 months. Set on the course or the learning rule, with an organisation default for mandatory learning. Some learning never expires.

W​

TermDefinition
Whole organisationEveryone in your organisation. Organisation administrator, Course manager and HR manager always cover the whole organisation; Learning manager and Report viewer can too.
