# Understand mandatory and recommended learning

Every assignment is either **Mandatory** or **Recommended**. This classification belongs to the assignment or learning
rule, never to the course itself, so the same course can be mandatory for some people and recommended for others. It's
for anyone who assigns or reports on learning.

1. Know what mandatory means

   **Mandatory** learning is required. It counts towards compliance, so it can be **Compliant**, **Due**, **Overdue** or
   **Expired**. People get reminders before and after the due date, and if your organisation has turned on manager
   escalation, their line manager is told when it stays overdue.

2. Know what recommended means

   **Recommended** learning is suggested and shows in the person's My learning. It's never overdue, and its due date is
   optional. Courses people enrol themselves in are recommended.

3. Know what "Not set" means

   **Not set** marks assignments made before classification existed. They're treated as mandatory for compliance,
   reminders and escalation.

4. Know which one is preselected

   When you assign a course, the form preselects your organisation's default classification. You can change it each
   time. Organisation administrators and HR managers set the default in **Learning rules** ▸ **Settings**.

5. Know what happens when sources overlap

   A person can be given the same course by several sources, such as a learning rule, an administrator and HR. They
   still have one assignment. It's mandatory if any source says so, and due by the earliest mandatory due date. The
   assignment's details list every source under **Why they have it**.
