View learning assignments

Quick answer

  • Open Manage ▸ Learning assignments.
  • Choose whose learning to see under Show people in, then filter by Course, Status, Mandatory or recommended and Assigned by.
  • Each row shows the person, course, status, classification, due date and who assigned it.
  • Use the row menu to View details, Change due date or Remove.

Learning assignments is one list of everyone's learning: who has which course, how far they've got and when it's due. You need the Learning manager, Report viewer, HR manager or Organisation administrator role. Report viewers can look but not change anything.

  1. Open Learning assignments

    Click Manage in the header, then Learning assignments under Organisation.

  2. Choose whose learning to see

    Under Show people in, choose an organisation. HR managers and organisation administrators start on Whole organisation; learning managers and report viewers start on the first organisation they're responsible for. Tick or clear Include child organisations to include or leave out the schools and departments below it.

  3. Filter the list

    Type a person or course in Search. Choose a Course, a Status (Not started, In progress, Overdue, Due in the next 30 days or Completed), Mandatory or recommended (Mandatory, Recommended or Not set) and Assigned by (Learning rule, Admin, Bulk assignment, HR or Self-enrolled). Changing the organisation clears the course filter.

  4. Read each row

    Person links to the person's details; a Group tag means a group assignment. Course shows "Renewal 1", "Renewal 2" and so on under renewed learning. Status is a coloured tag, with Recorded in HR when the completion came from your HR system. Classification shows Mandatory, Recommended or "Not set". Due shows the date and a note such as "Due in 5 days", "Due today" or "Overdue". Recommended learning past its date shows "Due date passed" because it's never overdue. Assigned by shows who or what assigned it.

  5. Act on a row

    Click the row menu to View details. HR managers and organisation administrators also see Change due date. Remove appears for people who can remove assignments. Click Refresh list to reload.
