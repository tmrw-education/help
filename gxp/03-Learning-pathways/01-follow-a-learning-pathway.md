# Follow a learning pathway

{/* PO REVIEW: see gxp-help-reference/features/learning-pathways.md, open questions 1 and 2. The learner page does not yet
mark completed courses as Done, and Locked courses can still be opened. Confirm the behaviour before publishing. */}

A learning pathway sets courses in an order, in steps, leading to a certification such as an induction programme.

1. Open Learning pathways

   Click **Learning pathways** in the header. Each pathway has a card with its title, its certification and a short
   description. The footer shows your progress and the course to do next.

2. Open a pathway

   Click the pathway's card. It opens to show the steps, and moves to the top of the page. Click it again to close it.

   ![The New starter induction pathway open, showing Next up, the key and the steps](./images/gxp-follow-a-learning-pathway-1.png)

3. Pick the next course

   **Next up** (or **Continue**, for a course you've started) names the course to do now. Click **Start course** or
   **Continue course**. If more courses are ready, the card says how many.

4. Or choose from the steps

   The steps run from **Step 1** downwards, and lines link each course to the ones it builds on. Click any course to open
   its details.

5. Launch the course

   In the course details, click **Launch course**. Work through it as usual; your progress also shows in **My courses**.

When you've completed every course in the pathway, and given any feedback that's required, you receive the pathway's
certificate automatically if it has one. It appears in **Certificates**.
